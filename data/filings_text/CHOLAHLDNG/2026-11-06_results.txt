                       CHOLAMANDALAM FINANCIAL HOLDINGS LIMITED (CFHL)

           UNAUDITED FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THE QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2026

CONSOLIDATED PROFIT AFTER TAX (PAT) FOR THE QUARTER ENDED SEPTEMBER 30, 2026 - Rs.1,520 Cr

Chennai, November 6, 2026: The Board of Directors of CFHL today approved the unaudited financial
results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Consolidated Results

Consolidated total income for the quarter ended September 30,2026 is Rs. 11,388 Cr as against Rs.9,589
Cr in the corresponding quarter of the previous year, registering a growth of 19%.

For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, the Company has achieved consolidated PAT of Rs. 1,520 Cr
as against Rs.1,214 Cr in the corresponding quarter of the previous year, registering a growth of 25%.

Cholamandalam Investment & Finance Company Ltd (CIFCL), in which the Company holds about
43.48% stake, disbursed Rs. 35,872 Cr in Q2 FY27 as against Rs. 24,442 Cr in Q2 FY26, registering a
growth of 47%.

PAT for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 is Rs.1,630 Cr compared to Rs.1,155 Cr in the
corresponding quarter of the previous year, registering a growth of 41%.

Assets under management grew by 23% to Rs. 2,68,009 Cr as at September 30, 2026 as compared to
Rs.2,14,906 Cr as at September 30, 2025.

Cholamandalam MS General Insurance Company Ltd., (CMSGICL) a subsidiary in general insurance
business, in which the Company holds about 60% stake, registered a Gross Written Premium (GWP) of
Rs. 4,618 Cr in H1 FY27 as against Rs. 4,351 Cr in the corresponding period of the previous year,
registering a growth of 6%. In Q2 FY 27 the company recorded a GWP of Rs.2,429 Cr as against Rs. 2,278
Cr in Q2 FY 26 with a growth of 7%.

For the six months ended Sep 30, 2026 CMSGICL registered a PAT of Rs.8 Cr as against profit of Rs.165 Cr
in the corresponding period of the previous year. For the quarter ended Sep 30, 2026 CMSGICL incurred
a post-tax loss of Rs.120 Cr as against a PAT of Rs.46 Cr in the corresponding period of the previous year.
Decline in profit is on account of NATCAT events in H1 FY’27 and elevated claim experience in Motor OD
segment.

CMSGICL has an investment book of Rs.18,937 crores as of 30 Sep 2026.

Cholamandalam MS Risk Services Ltd., a Joint Venture Company, in which the Company holds 49.5%
stake, registered total income of Rs. 26.32 Cr for the quarter ended Sep 30, 2026 as against Rs.22.89 Cr
in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

PAT for the quarter ended June 30, 2026 is Rs.0.85 Cr as against Rs. 1.45 Cr in the corresponding period
of the previous year


Standalone Results

The total income of the company for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 is Rs. 31 Cr as against Rs.
30.07 Cr in the corresponding quarter of the previous year.

PAT for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 is Rs.27.89 Cr as against Rs.27.16 Cr in the corresponding
quarter of the previous year.
