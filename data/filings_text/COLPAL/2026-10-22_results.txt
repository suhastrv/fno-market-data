Mumbai, 22 October 2026: Colgate-Palmolive (India) Limited announced its financial results
for the second quarter ended September 30, 2026.

for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Net Sales increased to Rs. 1,692 Crore from Rs.
1,507 Crore for the same period last year. The Company reported Net profit after tax of Rs. 342
Crore as compared to Rs. 328 Crore for the same period last year. Excluding one-offs and
exceptional items, Net Profit after tax grew by 5.5% year-on-year.

Mr. Manish Anandani, Managing Director & CEO of Colgate-Palmolive (India) Limited, said,

“We are delighted to report double-digit topline growth for the second consecutive quarter.
This consistent performance reflects broad-based growth momentum across our brands and
distribution channels. The Toothpaste portfolio delivered high-single digit volume expansion,
led by our Premium Toothpaste range and strong performance in Colgate MaxFresh.

Driven by a consistent focus on the Funding the Growth program, gross margin expanded by
40 bps year-on-year to 69.6% in Q2. We remain committed to navigating commodity cost
fluctuations through disciplined cost savings and calibrated pricing actions. Capitalising on
this healthy and competitive margin profile, we further stepped up investments in brand
building & premiumisation throughout the quarter. Looking ahead, we expect to deliver robust
growth, anchored by a best-in-class science-backed product portfolio tailored across consumer
benefits and reinforced by strategic advertising investments.”
