Revenue grew 20% year-on-year to INR15,600 million, crossing INR15,000 million in quarterly
revenue for the first time in our history. EBITDA grew 21%, while PAT increased 39% to
INR1,818 million. This also marks our eighth consecutive quarter of sequential revenue growth.
These results were delivered despite a challenging operating environment. Geopolitical
developments continue to put pressure on input and logistics costs across the industry.


We expect some of these pressures to persist with customer price adjustments following with a
lag. Against this backdrop, the consistency of our performance gives us confidence in the
underlying strength and momentum of the business. In our September investor presentation, I
spoke about our return to growth as the beginning and beginning to a new journey.


Our growth strategy has multiple pillars: controlled substances under our Make in America
platform, complex generics and early market opportunities, geographical expansion, oncology
and peptide CDMO. The progress across our business is beginning to validate this direction.


North America, our largest market, continued to grow, led by controlled substances. Europe
sustained its growth momentum, while our efforts to develop the rest of the world markets are
beginning to contribute. Our peptide CDMO business through Senn also grew year-on-year. This
platform remains in its build-out phase with a near-term focus on strengthening execution,
customer delivery and profitability. One of the most significant changes within Granules is the
evolution of our R&D and product development strategy.


We are pursuing differentiated opportunities, addressing greater technical complexity and
building the potential for early market entry. Our first-to-file strategy is a visible outcome of this
transformation. Over the last 3 quarters, we have announced filings for 3 potential sole first-to-
file ANDAs, generic DYANAVEL XR or Amphetamine-based extended-release OTT, Sodium
oxybate ER and Ruxolitinib ER. Several more such products are under development with filings
targeted over the next 6 to 7 quarters. These opportunities have a different commercial profile
from conventional generics.


Successful early entry, particularly into products with significant technical or regulatory barriers
can create substantial value. The timing and extent of this contribution will depend on regulatory
and litigation outcomes and the upside may be uneven across quarters. Alongside these
opportunities, we have 26 ANDAs awaiting approval, providing a strong pipeline for the next
wave of launches.


On sustainability, we continue to make steady progress through our SEDEX assessments and
CRISIL scores. 4 of our facilities have also achieved zero waste to landfill certification. We
remain focused on making these improvements an integral part of how we operate.


Granules today is fundamentally different from the company we were a few years ago. We have
a broad portfolio, deeper development capabilities and multiple avenues for growth. Our focus
remains on disciplined execution, strengthening quality, delivering reliability to customers and
translating our investments into sustained growth and earnings.
