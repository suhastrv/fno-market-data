Speaking about margins for the current year, we witnessed a gross margin contraction of
approximately 400 basis points year-on-year, primarily driven by an increase in raw material
prices, particularly the solvents, amidst ongoing geopolitical uncertainties and broader supply
chain disruptions. We believe these pressures are temporary in nature and have impacted our
quarterly performance.


However, on a full-year basis, we expect to recover the margin impact to an improvement in
revenue coupled with effective cost optimization measures and optimum inventory management.


Margins were also impacted by higher power and fuel cost. This was partly offset by the
renewable energy sources in which we invested last year. As power and fuel costs stabilize, we
expect to realize the full benefits of these renewable energy investments providing further
support to our overall margin profile.


On the EBITDA front, our reported margins stood at 33.8% in Q2 FY ‘27. For H1 FY ‘27,
EBITDA margins remained fairly stable at 33.0% compared to 33.2% in H1 FY ‘26, despite the

of our business model.

yet to ramp up, our H1 FY ‘27 EBITDA margin would have been approximately 37.8%, closer
to our historical margin levels. As these investments begin to contribute to revenue and the input
cost environment normalizes, we expect our margins to progressively improve.
