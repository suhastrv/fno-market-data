     Basis of Qualified Conclusion

5.   We draw attention to Note 5 of the Statement with reference to Rajasthan Sun Technique Energy Private
     Limited (RSTEPL), a wholly owned subsidiary company, wherein the auditors of RSTEPL have
     modified their review conclusion on its financial results for the quarter and half year ended September
     30, 2026. They have set out that RSTEPL has incurred a net loss of Rs. 11,952 lakhs during the half year
     ended September 30, 2026 respectively, as well as during the preceding financial years, and has a
     negative net worth of Rs. 2,72,954 lakhs as at September 30, 2026. Further, as at September 30, 2026, its
     current liabilities exceeded its current assets by Rs. 2,55,346 lakhs, and RSTEPL has defaulted in
     repayment of borrowings aggregating to Rs. 2,33,547 lakhs. These events and conditions indicate the
     existence of a material uncertainty that may cast significant doubt about RSTEPL' s ability to continue as
     a going concern. The appropriateness of the going concern assumption is critically dependent upon
     RSTEPL's ability to raise finance and generate cash flows in future to meet its obligations. However, the
     financial results of RSTEPL have been prepared on a going concern basis for the reasons stated in the
     aforesaid note. In the absence of necessary evidence with respect to management's assessment of going
     concern, the auditors of RSTEPL were unable to obtain adequate evidence regarding management's use
     of the going concern assumption in the preparation of the financial results of RSTEPL.

6.   We draw attention to Note 6 of the Statement wherein the auditors ofSamalkot Power Limited (SMPL)
     have set out that SMPL has incurred a net loss of Rs. 6,231 lakhs during the half year ended September
     30, 2026 respectively, as well as during the preceding financial years, and has a negative net worth of
     Rs. 2,89,415 lakhs as at September 30, 2026. Further, as at September 30, 2026, its current liabilities
     exceeded its current assets by Rs. 4,62,989 lakhs, and SMPL has defaulted in repayment of borrowings
     aggregating to Rs. 1,89,239 lakhs. The lender of SMPL has invoked the corporate guarantee against the
     Parent Company and filed an application under section 7 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016,
     and the arbitration initiated by SMPL was withdrawn on July 30, 2026 following the Arbitration
     Tribunal's partial final award on jurisdiction dated June 23, 2026. These events and conditions indicate
     the existence of a material uncertainty that may cast significant doubt about SMPL's ability to continue
     as a going concern. However, the financial results of SMPL have been prepared on a going concern basis
     for the reasons stated in the aforesaid note, including the proposed sale of equipment of Module 2 and
     Module 3, which is subject to the lender's approval. In the absence of necessary evidence with respect to
     management's assessment of going concern, the auditors of SMPL were unable to obtain adequate
     evidence regarding management's use of the going concern assumption in the preparation of the financial
     results of SMPL.

   jurisdiction, holding that it does not have jurisdiction in respect of SMPL's claim. The Arbitration
   Tribunal expressly confined its decision to the issue of jurisdiction and did not examine or determine

     Ratios have been computed as under:
      • Debt Service Coverage Ratio = Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation, other non-cash
         operating expenses and exceptional items/ (Interest on Long Term and Short-term Debt for
        the period/year + Principal Repayment of Long -term Debt for the period/ year).
      • Interest Service Coverage Ratio= Earnings before interest, tax, depreciation , other non-cash
        operating expenses and exceptional items/ Interest on Long Term and Short-term Debt for
        the period/year.
      • Debt Equity Ratio = Total Debt / Equity (excluding capital reserve, share based payment
         reserve and equity instrument measured at fair value through other comprehensive income
         (FVTOCI) reserve).
      • Current Ratio = Current Assets/Current Liabilities
      • Long-term Debts to Working Capital = non-current borrowing including current
         maturities/working capital excluding current maturities of non-current borrowings
      • Bad debts to Account Receivable ratio = Bad debts/Average Trade Receivable
      • Current Liability Ratio = Total Current Liabilities/Total Liabilities
      • Total Debts to Total Assets = Total Debts/Total Assets
      • Debtors Turnover Ratio= Revenue from Operation/Average Trade Receivable
      • Operating margin= Operating Profit/(Loss) / Revenue from operation
      • Net profit margin= Profit/(Loss) after tax (excluding exceptional items)/Total Income
      • Basic EPS = Profit/ (loss) after tax/ Weighted average No's of equity shares
      • Diluted EPS = Profit/ (loss) after tax/ (Weighted average No's of equity shares + outstanding
         share warrants)
