 Pune, Novembe r 7th, 2026


 Finolex Cables Ltd., (FCL) at the meeting of its Board of Directors held today approved

 for the corresponding period in year 2025-26, representing a 65%o growth in value terms. In
 volume terms, Electrical segment revenue grew significantly in automobile, agricultural,
 industrial and solar applications. Within Communication Cables segment, Optic Fiber
 Cable volumes were significantly higher than the previous year and with higher
 realizations, margins were also high.

 previous year, an improvement of 53%o.

 new the plant (4 million fiber kilometers) is now expected to be commenced in FY27.
