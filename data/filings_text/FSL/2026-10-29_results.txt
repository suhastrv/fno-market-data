Note on segment information
Business segments

Operating segments are defined as components of an enterprise for which discrete financial information is available that is evaluated regularly by the Chief Operating Decision Maker (‘CODM’), in
deciding how to allocate resources and in assessing performance. Operating segments are identified based on the internal organization at the Balance Sheet date. With the objective of internal
financial reporting and decision making of the Group, the CODM has reviewed the manner in which the Group views the business risks and returns and monitors its operations. Accordingly, the group
has identified business segment which comprises of Banking and Financial Services, Healthcare, Communication, Media and Technology and Diverse Industries.

Revenues and expenses directly attributable to the segments are reported under each reportable segment. The accounting principles used in the preparation of the segment information are
consistently applied to record revenue and expenditure in individual business segments.
Assets and liabilities used in the Group’s business are not directly identified to any of the operating segments, as these are used interchangeably between segments. Allocation of such assets and
liabilities is not practicable and any forced allocation would not result in any meaningful segregation. Hence assets and liabilities have not been identified to any of the reportable segments by the
Group.

       Firstsource Solutions Reports Second Quarter Fiscal 2027 Results
         Revenue grew by 22% YoY; Tenth straight quarter of double-digit YoY revenue growth;
          Raises lower end of FY27 constant currency revenue growth guidance to 10.5%-13%


Mumbai, October 29, 2026: Firstsource Solutions Limited (NSE: FSL, BSE: 532809), a global intelligence
partner and an RP-Sanjiv Goenka Group company, reported its consolidated financial results for the quarter and
half year ended September 30, 2026, according to IndAS.

Financial highlights for Quarter ended September 30, 2026
•    Revenues at ₹ 28,256 million (US$ 296 million), up 22.2% YoY
•    EBIT at ₹ 3,521 million or 12.5% of revenues, up 32.1% YoY
•    PAT at ₹ 2,328 million or 8.2% of revenue, up 29.7% YoY
•    Diluted EPS of ₹ 3.31

Financial highlights for Half Year ended September 30, 2026
•    Revenues at ₹ 55,505 million (US$ 584 million), up 22.5% YoY
•    EBIT at ₹ 6,888 million or 12.4% of revenues, up 33.4% YoY
•    Adjusted PAT* at ₹ 4,551 million or 8.2% of revenue; and reported PAT at ₹ 3,987 million
•    Diluted EPS of ₹ 5.66
* Adjusted for exceptional charges of ₹ 563 million, net of tax, recognized in Q1FY27.

Dr. Sanjiv Goenka, Chairman - RPSG Group and Firstsource Solutions, commented, “Firstsource’s first-
half results, with twelve consecutive quarters of sequential growth and eight consecutive quarters of margin
expansion, reflect the strength of its strategy and the discipline with which it is being executed. As AI reshapes
our industry, the company is moving beyond traditional outsourcing to build a business around technology-led
solutions, deep domain expertise and accountability for outcomes. Its twenty-five-year foundation is being
translated into productized capabilities that can scale intelligence across complex business processes. This is
how Firstsource is positioning itself for the next phase of growth, by strengthening its competitive advantage,
deepening client relationships and building a business capable of delivering sustained, profitable growth.”

About Firstsource
Firstsource Solutions Limited, an RP-Sanjiv Goenka Group company (NSE: FSL, BSE: 532809), is a global intelligence
partner to enterprises across healthcare, banking and financial services, communications, media, technology, retail, and
utilities. Its inch-wide, mile-deep practitioners work collaboratively to reimagine business process management. With
operations across the US, UK, India, Philippines, Mexico, Romania, Trinidad & Tobago, South Africa, and Australia,

advantage for the world’s most regulated industries. (www.firstsource.com)
