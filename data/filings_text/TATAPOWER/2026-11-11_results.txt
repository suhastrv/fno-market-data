        2.    The Company supplied power from the Mundra Power Plant ("Plant") upto 30th June 2025, based on the
              directions of the Ministry of Power ('MoP') under Section 11 of the Electricity Act, 2003, since 16th April, 2023 .
              Effective 3rd July 2025, the Company temporarily suspended operations of the Plant to undertake pending
              overhauling activities aimed at resolving existing technical issues. On 23rd March 2026 , with effect from 1st
              April 2025, the Company executed the supplementary power purchase agreement ('SPPA') with Gujarat Urja
              Vikas Nigam Limited ('GUVNL'), with revised tariff and power supply framework, which superseded the earlier
              PPA for GUVNL's contracted capacity. While approvals from the remaining procurers are in progress, the MoP
              issued directions under Section 11 on 22nd March 2026, permitting plant operations from 1st April 2026 to
              30th June 2026 with the terms of SPPA which was extended up to 30th September 2026 by way of directions
              issued on 19th June 2026. Further on 25th September 2026, such directions have been extended till 31st
              December 2026. Management expects completion of the SPPA with the other procurers during this period.

        3.    On 26th September 2023 the Singapore International Arbitration Centre ('SIAC') published a liability award
              whereby the SIAC held that the Company was in breach of certain clauses of the Non-disclosure agreements
              entered into with Kleros Capital Partners Limited ('Kleros') and of its contractual duty of good faith and
              confidence. Further, on 1st July 2025 and 27th August 2025, the SIAC published a quantum award and final
              award (together referred to as 'Awards'), directing the Company to pay Kleros damages for loss of opportunity
              of USO 490.32 million with simple interest of 5.33% from 30th November 2020 and cost of SGD 11.34 million
              with simple interest of 5.33% from 1st July 2025.

              On 23rd October 2025, the Company had challenged the Awards before the Singapore International
              Commercial Court ('SICC') by filing applications to set aside the Awards . However, on 26th August 2026, the
              SICC dismissed the Company's applications to set aside these Awards ('SICC Award'), concluding that the
              SIAC had considered and determined the relevant issues and that there was no basis for the SICC to re-
              examine those matters. Further, the SICC passed judgments on 17th September 2026 and 18th September
              2026, for enforcement of the Quantum Award and the Final Award respectively. The Company has filed notices
              of appeal against the SICC Award before the Singapore Court of Appeal ('Apex Court') on 7th September 2026
              which were accepted on 15th September 2026. Pursuant to the directions issued by the Court, the Company
              is required to file its written submissions by 23rd November 2026, Kleros is required to file its submissions by
              14th December 2026, and the Company is required to file its reply submissions by 24th December 2026. The
              appeal has been listed for hearing on 19th January 2027.

              Subsequently, in an ex parte application filed by Kleros before the SICC, an order dated 26th October, 2026
              was passed directing officers of the Company to appear before the Registrar on 8th December 2026 and
              provide details of the Company's assets. Pursuant thereto, on 6th November 2026, notices were served on
              the Company's wholly owned Singapore subsidiaries , Tata Power International Pte. Limited and Shira
              Investments Pte. Limited , intimating attachment of the Company's shareholding in the aforesaid entities by the
              Court-appointed Sheriff (Enforcement Officer) upto the value of SGD 830 .77 million. The Sheriff also served a
              copy of the aforesaid order passed by the SICC. The Company's shareholding in these entities is pledged in
