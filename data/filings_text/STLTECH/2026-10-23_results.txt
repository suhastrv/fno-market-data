PRESS RELEASE

STL achieves record Q2 FY’27, with highest-ever revenue, EBITDA and
open order book
    •    Record order book of INR 35,274 Cr and highest-ever revenue of INR 2,529 Cr
    •    Signed multi-year long-term supply agreements (LTSAs) with hyperscalers and telecom operators

Mumbai, 23/October/2026: STL [NSE: STLTECH], a leading connectivity solutions provider for AI-ready digital
infrastructure, today announced its financial results for the quarter ended 30 September 2026. For Q2 FY’27, the
Company reported the highest-ever revenue of INR 2,529 Cr and EBITDA of INR 846 Cr, a YoY growth of ~145% and
~500%, respectively. The open order book stands at INR 35,274 Cr at the end of the quarter.

This record revenue reflects the convergence of several factors. Demand for AI-ready digital infrastructure continues to
accelerate, with hyperscalers building out data centers that need far more fibre per rack than earlier cloud architectures.
A growing share of the business now comes from integrated optical connectivity solutions, with the optical connectivity
attach rate reaching an all-time high of 24% during H1 FY’27.

Large multi-year agreements with hyperscalers and telecom operators supported strong order intake and steady
execution through the quarter. STL also reported a net debt-free balance sheet this quarter, with PAT at INR 517 Cr,
driven by strong revenue growth, margin expansion and a one-time tariff refund gain.

Key order wins in H1 FY’27:

STL’s diversified order wins during H1 reflect growing demand for high-performance optical connectivity solutions
from hyperscalers and telecom operators globally:

    •    A US$ 1.2 Bn (~INR 11,500 Cr) multi-year long-term agreement with a hyperscaler to supply optical
         connectivity products from CY26 to CY30.
    •    A US$ 1.11 Bn (~INR 10,500 Cr) Product Award Letter from a hyperscaler to supply optical connectivity
         products from FY27 to FY29.
    •    A $288 Mn (~INR 2,700 Cr) deal with a hyperscaler for high-density optical fibre cable products from CY27 to
         CY29.
    •    A US$ 210 Mn (~INR 2,000 Cr) deal for a leading telecom infrastructure company for high-density optical fibre
         cables from CY27 to CY29.
    •    A multi-year optical cable supply agreement worth INR 960 Cr with a leading Indian telecom operator.

Innovation

STL continued to strengthen its portfolio of next-generation optical products in line with the needs of AI-ready digital
infrastructure, taking its total patent count to 800+:

    •    Expanded the STL Neuralis portfolio with Pre-Terminated Fibre Trunk Assemblies (48F–6,912F), designed to
         power hyperscale data centres.
    •    Launched a plenum-rated fibre trunk for AI data centre connectivity.
    •    Launched a new advanced FTTH solution, CONCAT, after successfully completing field trials on the networks of
         one of the largest telecom service providers in the US.

for AI-ready digital infrastructure” said Ankit Agarwal, MD, STL. “The large multi-year agreements we secured this
