             Coming to quarter two, we saw a healthy momentum across our India and international business.

             Our revenue from operations was INR4,520 crores, year-on-year growth of 13.8%. Biotech sales
             was INR 70 crores with year-on-year degrowth of 35%, but that's due to the lumpy business
             nature of the CDMO. MedTech sales were INR 133 crores. That includes Occlutech.


             EBITDA was INR1,025 crores, resulted in a EBITDA margin of 22.5%, a growth of 11.3% year-
             on-year. R&D expenses were 4.3% of our revenues. Profit before tax grew 7.7% year-on-year.
             Net profit saw a degrowth of 11.7%, and net cash stood at INR4,900 crores plus.


             According to IQVIA, we saw the company register a growth of 13.8% year-on-year, at par with
             the Indian pharmaceutical industry, which grew at 13.8% as well. Acute segment reported a
             growth of 12.1% at par with the IPM growth of 12.3%. Chronic segment reported a growth of
             22.4% versus the industry, which grew by 16%, 640 basis points outperformance. We have
             outperformed IPM in eight key focus therapies. In multivitamins by ~1.2x, anti-diabetic again

             CNS by 1.1x & Derma by 1.6x.
