   SAMIL established India's first professionally managed auction platform for pre-owned cars, commercial

  Backed by CarTrade Tech Limited and Shriram Finance Limited, we have a strong presence across India.
   Our network comprises over 1,600 employees dedicated to providing exceptional service and more than
   135 Automalls strategically located throughout India. We conduct thousands of auction events every month
   via Physical (Automalls), Phygital (MySAMIL App), and digital (cartradeexchange.com & bids.samil.in)
   platforms. Through gold.samil.in, we also facilitate secure and transparent gold auctions for banks and
   financial institutions.

   throughout the pre-owned asset lifecycle. Through Adroit Auto, we provide expert inspection, valuation, and
   claim settlement services for pre-owned vehicles and equipment. ThePriceX offers data-driven pricing
   solutions for pre-owned vehicles and equipment. Augeo facilitates the auction of properties, plant &
   machinery, commodities, scrap, salvage, and surplus assets.
