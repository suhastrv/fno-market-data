Looking at revenue composition and business performance, exports continue to constitute
approximately 90% of standalone sales revenue, reflecting the Company's strong international
business presence. Europe and North America together accounted for approximately 75% of the
standalone sales revenue. On a constant currency basis, standalone revenue for the first half
registered a year-on-year growth of 17%. In terms of business composition, generics contributed
38% of sales revenue during the first half, while custom synthesis accounted for the remaining
62%.


Our global nutraceutical business recorded revenue of ₹571 crores for the first half compared to
₹492 crores in the corresponding period of the previous financial year.


Net material consumption for the first half stood at approximately 32% of the sales revenue

foreign exchange movement, the Company recorded a net forex gain of ₹20 crores during the
first half compared to a gain of ₹103 crores in the corresponding period last year.
