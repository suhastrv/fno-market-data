Moving on to the industry outlook on Slides 12 to 14. On Slide 12, from a pricing perspective,

Looking at the global aluminium market in Q3 calendar year 2026, Chinese aluminium
production grew about 3% year-on-year to nearly 11 million tons, supported by capacity
additions in Yunnan, partially offset by capacity rationalization in Shandong. In the rest of the

from the Middle East conflict and the closure of Mozal, partially offset by higher production in
Indonesia. As a result, the global aluminium production stood at approximately 19 million
tons, down 1% year-on-year.


On the demand side, aluminium consumption in China increased 4% to around 12 million tons,
supported by continued strength in new energy vehicles and healthy export demand for semi-
fabs and finished products. However, the construction sector remained weak amid ongoing
challenges in the real estate market. Outside China, consumption declined 2% to around 7
million tons, reflecting softer demand in Europe, Middle East, and North America, partially
offset by growth in India. Overall, the global aluminium consumption reached approximately
19 million tons, up 1% year-on-year. With demand continuing to outpace supply, global
aluminium markets remained in deficit by roughly 0.5 million tons this quarter.
