      Veicoli Sri, Italy ('"Veicoli''), a wholly-owned step-down subsidiary of the Company. The sale is aligned with the Group's strategic
      focus on its core auto components business. On 3rd August, 2026, upon receipt of the requisite regulatory approvals, Endurance
      Overseas SpA completed the transfer of its entire shareholding in Veicoli in accordance with SPA. Consequently, Veicoli ceased to
      be a subsidiary of Endurance Overseas SpA and, in tum, of the Company. The consideration comprised of an upfront fixed payment
      of €2.2 million, deferred fixed payment of €0.3 million and earn-out consideration linked to Veicoli's financial performance for the
      first three years following completion of the transaction, capped at €5.0 million, subject to agreed adjustments. The gain on sale of
      €1.14 million (~12.48 Crore) has been included under Other Income.


  4   On 29th June, 2026, pursuant to the terms of the Share Purchase Agreement executed in December 2024, Endurance Overseas SpA
      acquired an additional 8% equity stake in St6ferle GmbH and Stoferle Automotive GmbH, Germany ('"St6ferle entities''), increasing
      its shareholding from 60% to 68%. The additional stake was acquired for a cash consideration of €6.24 million, determined in
      accordance with the valuation methodology agreed under the Share Purchase Agreement.
      Further, on 29th September, 2026, pursuant to an Amendment Agreement to the Share Purchase Agreement dated 12th December,
      2024, Endurance Overseas SpA acquired the remaining 32% equity stake in Stoferle entities, for an upfront cash consideration of €18
      million. As a result of this transaction, a deferred consideration liability of €3.24 million (~35.63 Crore), which was no longer to be
      paid, was derecognized, and the resulting gain has been disclosed as an exceptional item.
      Consequently, Endurance Overseas SpA's shareholding in the Stoferle entities increased from 68% to 100%.
