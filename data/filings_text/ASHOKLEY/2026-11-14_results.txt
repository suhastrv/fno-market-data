                                  Ashok Leyland Delivers Robust Q2 Performance
                                    with Record Volumes, Revenue and Profit
                                          Volume up 32%, Revenue up 39%, PBT up 14%
                                                Declares 100% Interim Dividend


        Chennai, November 14, 2026: Ashok Leyland, the Indian flagship of the Hinduja Group,
        delivered a strong performance in the second quarter, achieving all-time high in commercial
        vehicle volumes, revenue and profits, reflecting sustained business momentum and
        operational strength.

          Commercial vehicle (CV) volumes surged 32% to 64,677 units, compared with 49,116 units in
          the corresponding quarter last year. Revenue grew by an impressive 39% to 'U3,364 crore,
        . up from ~9,588 crore in the previous year, driven by strong market demand and better price
          realisation and mix.

        EBITDA stood at ~ 1,350 Cr (10.1%), compared with ~1,162 Cr (12.1%) in the corresponding
        period last year. PBT for the quarter reached U,187 Cr, compared with "! 1,043 Cr growing at
        14% over the same period last year, reflecting a significant improvement in financial
        performance. Profit After Tax (PAT) stood at ~869 crore, compared with U71 crore in the
        corresponding quarter last year, registering 13% growth.

        Both the Medium and Heavy Commercial Vehicle (MHCV) and Light Commercial Vehicle (LCV)
        industry witnessed healthy growth during Q2. Ashok Leyland outperformed the industry in
        the MHCV segm ent, registering an impressive 44% year-on-year growth in volumes to 37,958
        units, compared with 26,307 units in the corresponding quarter last year, against indu stry
        growth of 39%. In the LCV segment, volumes grew by 21% to 21,371 units, up from 17,697
        units in the previous year. The domestic MHCV truck volumes surged 48% significantly
        outpacing industry growth of 41%.

        Our strong performance translated into a domestic truck market share of 31.5%, representing
        a gain· of 1.5% during the quarter (excludes Defence). Ashok Leyland also maintained its
        market share in the bu s segment (excludes EV bu ses). In the LCV bu siness, VAHAN market
        share in the addressable segments improved to 13.6%, reflecting a year-an-year gain of 0.3%.

        Export volumes for the quarter stood at 5,102 units, registering a 7% year-on-year increase.

        The Defence, Power Solution s and Aftermarket businesses continu ed to deliver strong
        performances and are well -positioned to sustain their growth mom entum through the
        current fin anci al ye ar.
