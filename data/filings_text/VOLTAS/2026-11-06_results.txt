Voltas Delivers Steady Q2 FY27 Performance, on the back of market leadership and Execution Excellence


Mumbai, 6 November 2026: The Board of Directors of Voltas Limited, India's No. 1 Air Conditioning brand from
the Tata Group, announced the consolidated financial results (including the consolidated segment report) of the
Company for the quarter ended 30 September 2026.

Voltas continued to reinforce its leadership in the Indian Room Air Conditioner (RAC) market during Q2 FY27, with
the RAC business recording 32% volume growth during the quarter. The Company maintained its No. 1 position in
the industry, achieving a market share of 17.4% for the year to date ended September 2026, with a 4.2% lead over
its nearest competitor. The performance reflects the continued strength of the Voltas brand, its product portfolio,
extensive distribution network and sustained focus on market execution.

The Unitary Cooling Products business recorded 29% revenue growth during the quarter, led by RAC, while
improving profitability despite commodity and currency related cost pressures. Voltbek continued to expand its
presence in the home appliances market, achieving record Q2 sales and further strengthening its market position
in Washing Machines and Refrigerators.

The Electro-Mechanical Projects and Services business maintained order momentum, supported by an order pad
exceeding ₹6,800 crore, providing revenue visibility. The Engineering Products and Services segment registered
30+% growth, supported by Mining and Construction Equipment and Textile Machinery.

Across its businesses, Voltas continued to focus on market competitiveness, product innovation, distribution
expansion, cost optimisation and disciplined execution, while strengthening the foundations for sustainable and
profitable growth.

Consolidated Results for the quarter ended 30th September,2026:

For the quarter ended 30th September,2026, Voltas recorded Consolidated Total Income of ₹ 2,746 crores,
compared to ₹ 2,412 crores in the same period last year. Profit Before Tax of ₹ 83 crores vs ₹ 54 crores in Q2 FY26.
Profit After Tax of ₹ 48 crores compared to ₹ 32 crores last year.

Consolidated Segment Results for the quarter and year ended 30th September 2026:

A. Unitary Cooling Products (UCP)

The Unitary Cooling Products business recorded 29% revenue growth in Q2 FY27, driven primarily by the Room
Air Conditioner business which materially outperformed the broader industry.

Voltas continued to consolidate its position as India's No. 1 Room Air Conditioning brand, maintaining a market
share of 17.4% for the year to date ended September 2026. The Company sustained a 4.2% lead over its
nearest competitor, reflecting its established market presence, consumer preference and distribution
strength.

The performance was supported by the Company's comprehensive product portfolio, continued product
innovation, effective channel engagement and an expanding distribution footprint. Voltas maintained its focus
on addressing evolving consumer preferences through relevant product offerings, technology enhancements
through its new AI range of split ACs and greater accessibility across markets.

The Company's leadership in RAC continues to be supported by its extensive distribution and service network,
brand recall and sustained investment in product development and consumer engagement. These capabilities
remain central to its strategy of consolidating market leadership while expanding its presence across
geographies and consumer segments.

Commercial Air Conditioning continued to deliver stable growth, while Home Comfort products registered
healthy growth during the quarter. Commercial Refrigeration recorded a flat quarter.

Despite industry wide pressures arising from commodity inflation and adverse currency movements, the
Unitary Cooling Products business improved profitability through calibrated pricing actions and ongoing cost
optimisation initiatives. These included localisation, sourcing efficiencies, product redesign and improvements
in manufacturing productivity.

The business remains focused on sustaining its competitive position, strengthening product differentiation,
expanding distribution reach and improving operating efficiencies. With a comprehensive product portfolio,
healthy inventory position and expanding market presence, Voltas continues to pursue opportunities for
market expansion and profitable growth.

Voltbek: Continued Expansion in Home Appliances

Voltbek delivered another quarter of growth, achieving record Q2 sales, supported by an improving product
mix, market share gains and continued expansion of its distribution network.

The business continued to strengthen its presence in the Indian home appliances market, achieving year to date
market shares of 8.8% in Washing Machines and 7.0% in Refrigerators.

Voltbek has continued to expand its scale through a wider product portfolio including a new line up of Frost
Free Refrigerators and Fully Automatic Washing Machines, sharper product segmentation, enhanced consumer
engagement and greater distribution penetration. The business remains focused on building relevance across
consumer segments through innovation, competitive product offerings and improved market accessibility.

Product development remains an important priority, supported by sourcing efficiencies and portfolio
optimisation initiatives aimed at strengthening competitiveness and improving profitability over time.
With its expanding presence in Washing Machines and Refrigerators, Voltbek continues to contribute to the
diversification of the Voltas consumer durables portfolio, enhance Voltas’ channel relevance complementing
the Company's established leadership in cooling products and making Voltas an all season consumer durable
brand.

B. Electro-Mechanical Projects and Services

The Electro-Mechanical Projects and Services business continued to maintain order momentum, supported by
opportunities across infrastructure, industrial and institutional projects.

Domestic Projects recorded order wins across industrial infrastructure, electronics manufacturing, metro and
tunnel projects, and data centres. The business maintained its focus on selective order booking, execution
discipline, working capital management and project profitability. The Company's approach remains centred on
pursuing opportunities that align with its technical capabilities, execution strengths and risk management
framework, while maintaining appropriate commercial discipline.

In the International Projects business, the favourable resolution of the Sidra Bank Guarantee matter eliminated
the related contingent liabilities, strengthening the risk profile of the business. The focus continued to remain
on disciplined project execution, prudent risk management and selective bidding.

As of 30 September 2026, the carry forward order book for the segment stood at over ₹6,800 crore, providing
revenue visibility across Domestic and International Projects. The business remains focused on converting its
order book through disciplined execution, maintaining working capital efficiency and pursuing opportunities
that support sustainable profitability.

C. Engineering Products and Services

The Engineering Products and Services segment recorded robust revenue growth during Q2 FY27, supported by
performance across Mining and Construction Equipment and Textile Machinery.

The Mining and Construction Equipment business benefited from growth in crushing and screening equipment,
continued execution of operations and maintenance contracts, stable performance in African operations and
contributions from aftermarket and service businesses. The business continued to focus on expanding customer
relationships, improving service capabilities and strengthening its presence across equipment and aftermarket
opportunities.

The Textile Machinery division maintained growth despite a cautious industry environment, supported by
traction in spinning and post spinning solutions, after sales services, spinning accessories and customer support
offerings.
The segment continues to focus on strengthening customer engagement, improving operational efficiencies and
expanding its portfolio of products and services to support sustainable growth.


Commenting on the performance, Mr. Mukundan Menon C P, Managing Director, Voltas Limited, said:

“Q2 FY27 reflects the continued progress of Voltas across its businesses, led by our Room Air Conditioner business,
which recorded 32% volume growth during the quarter. With a 17.4% market share and a 4.2 percentage point lead
over our nearest competitor, we have further consolidated our position as India's No. 1 Room Air Conditioner brand.
The improvement in profitability of our Unitary Cooling Products business, despite input cost pressures, reflects our
focus on operational efficiencies and cost competitiveness. Voltbek continues to gain scale and market share, while
our Projects business maintains a healthy order book and disciplined execution. Our Engineering Products and
Services business also recorded robust growth, supported by momentum in Mining and Construction Equipment and
Textile Machinery.

Our priorities remain clear: strengthen market leadership, accelerate innovation, expand distribution, enhance
customer experience and drive sustainable, profitable growth.”

       In connection to the sub-contract, the Company had issued bank guarantees amounting to~ 437.99 crores (QAR 166.6 million) to the Main Contractor which had been disclosed as a
       contingent liability in the financial statements over the years. In June 2023, the Company was informed by its bankers that the Main Contractor had sought to invoke the said bank
       guarantees. However, due to certain deficiencies in the invocation process , the guarantees were not honoured by the Bank, leading to the commencement of legal proceedings. The
       Company, Main Contractor, Consortium partner and the Bank had filed their respective appeals before the Court of Appeal (Qatar). The Court of Appeal (Qatar), vide its judgment
       dated 04 May 2026, directed the Main Contractor to return the bank guarantees issued by the Company and to pay the settlement amount towards variation order claims and
       compensation to the Company and its Consortium Partner. Pursuant to the said judgement, the bank guarantees have been cancelled by the bank. Subsequently, the Main Contractor
       had filed an appeal before the Court of Cassation against the order of the Court of Appeal (Qatar). The Court of Cassation dismissed the appeal and upheld the judgment of the Court
       of Appeal (Qatar) vide its order dated 30 August 2026. Accordingly, the matter has attained finality. Consequently, the Company has carried out an assessment of provisions and
       payables in respect of the said project and has written back ~ 63.04 crores during the quarter ended 30 September 2026 which is included in other operating income.
