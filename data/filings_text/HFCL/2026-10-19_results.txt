HFCL Reports Best-Ever Quarterly Performance in Q2
FY27; Profits Rise Over 5x YoY
New Delhi, 19th October 2026: HFCL Limited (HFCL), a leading technology enterprise specialising in optical fiber,
optical fiber cable and connectivity solutions, telecom products, Defence and Aerospace products, and digital
infrastructure, today announced its best-ever quarterly performance for the second quarter and half year ended
September 30, 2026.
Q2 FY27 marked a strong improvement in both scale and profitability. Consolidated revenue increased to ₹2,541.79
crore from ₹1,043.34 crore in Q2 FY26, growing 143.62% year-on-year and 32.73% sequentially. The improvement
in profitability was even more remarkable, with both profit before tax and profit after tax growing more than
fivefold year-on-year. Consolidated profit before tax stood at ₹538.60 crore, up 406.49% year-on-year and 62.46%
quarter-on-quarter, while consolidated profit after tax reached ₹394.22 crore, registering growth of 448.14% year-
on-year and 60.49% quarter-on-quarter. On half yearly basis the PBT grew from ₹61.64 crore to ₹870.12 crore, up
by 1311.62% while PAT grew from ₹42.62 crore to ₹639.86 crore, up by 1401.31%
The performance reflects increasing scale, operating strength and momentum across HFCL’s businesses. The order
book now standing at ₹28,911 crore, together with advanced opportunity pipeline, provides multi-year revenue
visibility. Demand across AI infrastructure, hyperscale data centres and telecom networks is accelerating, while
international opportunities are steadily converting into firm contracts.

About HFCL
HFCL is a leading technology enterprise with operations in high end Optical Fiber and Optical Fiber Cables, Optical
Connectivity Solutions, Telecom and Defence Equipment. The Company is also creating digital networks for Telcos,
Enterprises and Defence Forces. Over the years, HFCL has emerged as a trusted partner offering sustainable high-
tech solutions with a commitment to providing the latest technological products to its customers. Our strong R&D
expertise coupled with our system integration services and decades of experience in Fiber optics enable us to
deliver innovative digital network solutions required for the most advanced networks.
The Company’s in-house R&D Centers located at Gurgaon, Bengaluru and Hyderabad along with invested R&D
houses and other R&D collaborators at different locations in India and abroad, innovate a futuristic range of
technology products and solutions. HFCL has developed capabilities to provide premium quality Optical Fiber and
Optical Fiber Cables, state-of-the-art Telecom Products including IP-MPLS Routers, Backhaul Radios, Wi-Fi Systems
and Defence products viz. Thermal Weapon Sights, Electronic Fuzes, High-Capacity Radio Relay and Surveillance
Radars of different variants and aerostructures.
The Company is now establishing a manufacturing facility in Andhra Pradesh to manufacture different ammunition
products for Defence sector as well as a preform manufacturing facility in Madhya Pradesh.
HFCL operates advanced manufacturing facilities in Hyderabad, Goa, Chennai (through its subsidiary HTL Limited)
and Hosur for defence equipment.
HFCL is a partner of choice for its customers across India, Europe, Asia Pacific, Middle East, Africa and USA. HFCL’s
commitment to quality and environmental sustainability inspires it to innovate solutions for the ever-evolving
customer needs.
