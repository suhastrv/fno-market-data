We expanded Axiom in modern trade chains and QuickCom, and we invested in building
visibility for the AloFrut brand. For IncNut, we are now expanding beyond D2C into
marketplaces. We have commenced operations on QuickCom, and we have also entered into
new international markets. When I step back and look at all of this together, both our core
portfolio and our strategic investments are growing strongly, and they are doing so side by side.


We are deliberately building a more diversified business, one that spans traditional FMCG,
personal and healthcare, and two, the premium beauty and grooming, digital-first brands, and
emerging consumer platforms. This diversification does two things. It widens our growth
runway, and it makes the business more resilient across different economic and consumption
cycles. Our channel transformation is moving at a steady pace. Organized channels now
contribute to 37% of our domestic business.


Modern trade and e-com both maintained strong momentum, and QuickCom now accounts for
around 15% of our digital sales. This is a significant shift in how our consumers are choosing to
shop with us. On our international business, Q2 performance was impacted by a few specific
external disruptions, with revenue declining by 2%. Importantly, the weakness was not broad-
based.


Nepal was impacted by severe floods, and Gulf markets continued to face supply chain
challenges arising from ongoing regional conflicts. Excluding Nepal and Iraq, our international
business grew by 6%. Inflationary pressures remained elevated through the quarter, and the
sharp rise in input costs weighed on our margins.


Together with the changing business mix following the integration of the acquired business, this
resulted in higher COGS and a moderation in gross margin by 520 basis points compared to last
year. Despite these pressures, EBITDA grew by 25% to INR222 crores, and profit before tax
grew by 29% to INR194 crores. I think this reflects the resilience of our business model and the
effectiveness of the cost management initiatives our teams undertook during the quarter.


Profit after tax stood at INR144 crores, lower by 3%, and that is largely due to the normalization
of our effective tax rate following the phased out of fiscal benefits. Looking at the first half of
FY27, revenue stood at INR2,127 crores, up 25%. EBITDA was at INR449 crores, up 15%, and
profit before tax INR388 crores, going by 15%.


On capital allocation, we completed our share buyback worth INR281 crores last month to buy
back 73 lakh shares. The Board has also approved the first interim dividend of INR5 per share,
which would amount to a payout of INR214 crores. Sorry. As we look ahead, we remain very
optimistic about the growth prospects of the business.


We are encouraged by the strong performance of our core brands, the rapid scaling of our digital-
first brands, and sustained traction in modern trade, e-com, and QuickCom. Commodity inflation
and geopolitical developments remain areas to watch for. However, our diversified portfolio,
strengthened distribution, robust innovation pipeline, and disciplined cost management position
us well to deliver sustained profitable growth through the remainder of FY27.


With that, I would now like to open the floor for questions. Thank you.
