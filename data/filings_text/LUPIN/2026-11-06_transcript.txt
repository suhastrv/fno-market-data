                Other Developed Markets. Importantly, this growth reflects the increasing
                strength and diversification of our business, with multiple markets

                momentum to continue into the second half of the year, with organic growth

                moderate in the coming quarters. This reflects intensifying competition in
                some of our key U.S. products, increased investment in our R&D pipeline, and
                higher input costs arising from the challenging macroeconomic environment
                in the Middle East.

                during the quarter. As anticipated, competition in Tolvaptan intensified with
                the entry of two additional generic players. While our base portfolio benefited

                were offset by pricing pressures in certain products, particularly Tolvaptan.
                We expect competitive intensity in Tolvaptan to increase further, with the
                possibility of additional entrants over the coming quarters.
                For FY27, we continue to expect U.S. revenues in the range of USD 1.1 billion
                to USD 1.2 billion. Growth in our base business, new injectable launches, and
                contributions from Pegfilgrastim are expected to help offset the impact of
                increasing competition in key products.

                Our priority is to double the share of complex products in our U.S. portfolio,
                with respiratory products, complex injectables, and biosimilars serving as key
                growth drivers. We are on track to file more than 15 products during the
