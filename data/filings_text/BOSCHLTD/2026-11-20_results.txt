 Note 4.   The above unaudited consolidated financial results were reviewed by the Audit Committee. The above unaudited consolidated

           required under Regulation 33 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, has been completed by
           the Statutory Auditors for the quarter and six months ended September 30, 2026 and they have issued an unqualified conclusion on
           the aforesaid results .
Note 5.a. On January 28, 2025, the Board of Directors of the Holding Company approved to execute the Business Transfer Agreement with
          Keenfinity India Private Limited ("the Purchaser") for transfer of its "Video solutions, Access and Intrusions and Communication
          systems" Business (Specified Business) for a consideration of Rs.5,950 million (excluding purchase price adjustment). The transfer
          of business was completed on May 01, 2025 and accordingly, the Holding Company has recognised a total gain on sale of the said
          Specified Business in the financial results amounting to Rs.5,560 million and the same has been disclosed as "exceptional item" in
          these unaudited consolidated financial results .

           The specified business did not qualify as a separate major line of business under IND AS 105 : Non current Assets held for sale and
           discontinued operations and accordingly was not considered as a "discontinued operation" for the purpose of these unaudited
           consolidated financial results .

Note 5.b. During the current quarter, the Group has decided to discontinue employee benefits towards service recognition for long term service
          for managerial staff, with effect from January 01, 2027. Accordingly, the provision towards long term service award amounting to
          Rs.1,304 million has been reversed and disclosed under "Exceptional Items" in these unaudited consolidated financial results for the
          quarter and six month ended September 30, 2026 .

Note 5.c. With effect from July 1, 2026, Bosch Limited ("RBIN ") acquired 100% of the equity shares of Bosch Chassis Systems India Private
          Limited ("RBIC") for a total consideration of Rs.90,238 million , after obtaining the requisite approvals. The aggregate purchase
          consideration of Rs.90 ,238 million was settled through a combination of cash consideration of Rs.90, 151 million and issuance of
          2,460 equity shares of RBIN valued at Rs.87 million including securities premium of Rs.86.97 million. Consequently, RBIC became a
          wholly owned subsidiary of RBIN. As RBIC was under the control of the same ultimate holding company both before and after the
          acquisition , the transaction constitutes a business combination under common control in accordance with Appendix C to Ind AS 103,
          Business Combinations. Accordingly, the business combination has been accounted for using the pooling of interests method, and
          the assets, liabilities and reserves of RBIC have been incorporated in these unaudited consolidated financial results at their existing
          carrying amounts.

           Effective April 1, 2025, RBIC revised its method of depreciation from the straight-line method to the diminishing balance method to
           align with the depreciation method followed by RBIN . Consequently, additional depreciation expense of Rs.775 million has been
           recognised in the comparative financial information for the six months ended September 30, 2025 included in these unaudited
           consolidated financial results . Accordingly, profit before tax for the six months ended September 30 , 2025 is lower by Rs.775 million.

           The comparative consolidated financial information for the quarter and six months ended September 30, 2025, as at and for the year
           ended March 31 , 2026 , and for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, together with the related information , has been restated as if the
           business combination had occurred on April 01 , 2025, in accordance with the requirements of Appendix C to Ind AS 103.
