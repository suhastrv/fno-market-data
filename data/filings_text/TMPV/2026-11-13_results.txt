its highest ever quarterly revenues at ₹20.4K Cr.

stood at ₹(5.5)K Cr post recognition of deferred tax asset. The Consolidated FCF was ₹(6.3)K Cr for Q2, resulting in Net Debt of ₹50.8K
Cr as at Sep 30, 2026.

Looking Ahead:
Global geopolitical volatilities, luxury segment demand trends, pace of EV adoption and commodity costs continue to be key
monitorable. We will continue to drive industry-beating growth for domestic business whilst improving margins through focused cost
reductions and calibrated pricing actions. For JLR, near term focus areas will be to build sales momentum from new EV launches and
demonstrate meaningful progress in Enterprise Missions plan. The businesses are focused on executing the long-term strategic plans.

weighed on margins. Both businesses remain fundamentally strong, and we stay focused on executing our medium-term priorities:
driving growth through new launches, accelerating cost reductions and improving cash generation.”
