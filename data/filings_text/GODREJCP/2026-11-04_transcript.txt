Aasif Malbari:   Good evening, everybody. FY27 has been a quarter of strong broad-based performance for
                 Godrej Consumer Products Limited, fully aligned with our expectations and strategic priorities.

                 despite subdued monsoon conditions in India, while being supported by tailwinds in Indonesia.


                 At a consolidated level, revenues grew 19% year-on-year, 15% in constant currency terms with
                 underlying volume growth of 8%. EBITDA grew 12%. Net profit grew 14% to INR523 crores.
                 This marks our second consecutive quarter of high teens revenue growth with high single-digit
                 underlying volume growth and double-digit profit growth.


                 Our stand-alone business delivered 13% sales growth performance, driven by 7% underlying
                 volume growth. This is despite an estimated 150 bps impact from trade inventory correction in
                 the quarter. While this has been a modest near-term impact in terms of the correction we've done,
                 we believe that maintaining healthy channel levels is important for operational excellence and
                 sales team to remain focused purely on offtake. Stand-alone EBITDA was mildly impacted due
                 to this correction.


                 In Home Care, we delivered 19% value growth led by strong performance in air fresheners and
                 home care liquids. Alongside that, we gained market share in Household Insecticides. This is
                 encouraging and indicates that the actions we have taken across brands are beginning to deliver.


                 Our electric portfolio delivered sequentially improving performance, supported by the new 6
                 minutes campaign, while incentives continue to scale strongly. Godrej Fab maintained a strong
                 double-digit growth trajectory and continued to strengthen its market position. Godrej Spic,
                 which was scaled nationally during Q1, continues to witness healthy consumer repeat and
                 sequential growth with significant distribution headroom still available.


                 During this quarter, we launched Godrej Rizz in selected markets across West and South India.
                 We also launched Godrej Zap, our entry into stain removers online. Both these new launches
                 have been received well.

                 increases and grammage reduction. although with a favorable comparator. Our premium liquids

Hair colors maintained its growth momentum and continues to gain market share across our key
bets. Perfumes and deodorants delivered strong double-digit growth, supported by healthy

Our international portfolio delivered strong growth. In Indonesia, sales grew 20% with

With our future portfolio of LV, Pocket, etcetera, we have seen tremendous success with double-

GUAM delivered another outstanding quarter. Sales grew 40% in INR terms and 23% in
constant currency terms, supported by underlying volume growth of 16%. EBITDA grew 43%.
This performance was broad-based across categories and geographies.


More importantly, GUAM has now delivered 7 consecutive quarters of double-digit top line
growth and 11 consecutive quarters of double-digit bottom line growth. This reinforces our
confidence that the improvements in the business is structural. We continue to be focused on the
larger opportunity that the continent offers. The launch of HI incense stick was well received
and is having good early traction.


The input cost environment remained significantly challenging through the quarter. Inflation
intensified across crude-linked derivatives, palm oil and commodity inputs. This led to one of
the highest inflationary impacts in the last many years.


Due to the nature of our portfolio in India and Indonesia, the impact of such inflation has been
approximately high single digit. Despite a significant portion of our portfolio being price point
pack, we have taken stringent pricing actions combined with large cost savings program and
media and trade level investment optimization. We strive to keep the share of voice range bound.


We have also increased the salience of our digital investments, which has given us much higher
ROI. We will continue to take calibrated pricing actions significantly in soaps and liquid
detergents, where the cost impact is large and the margins even today are below normative levels.


With continued cost savings and future pricing, we will ensure that we continue to invest behind
our growth drivers while managing to deliver on our stated profit growth guidance. We continue
to hold our FY27 guidance, high single-digit underlying volume growth in stand-alone and
double-digit revenue and EBITDA growth at a consol level. With revenues running ahead of
plan through the first half, we remain on track to deliver with the confidence to exceed it in
certain areas.


We remain confident in the resilience of our portfolio, the strength of our brands and our ability
to deliver sustained profitable growth going forward. Our measure of outperformance remains
double-digit UVG and profit growth. Thank you.
