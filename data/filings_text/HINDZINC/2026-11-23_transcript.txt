per annum.


This particular project also includes the earlier approved integrated zinc smelter at Debari and
other facilities. With an expected commission in next three to five years, the overall 2.0 growth
plan includes setting up of a 765 KTPA zinc smelter, 200 KTPA lead smelter, and 700 tons per
annum silver refining capacity.


All of this is backed by equivalent mining and concentration functions. The expansion takes the
overall integrated zinc capacity to 1.6 million tons, integrated lead capacity to 0.4 million tons,
and silver capacity to 1,500 tons per annum.


Maintaining the best operational efficiencies, leveraging economies of scale, and active adoption
of digitalization and automation to improve our process will lead to our zinc cost of production,
excluding royalty, to stay below $1,000 per ton, even after our expansion plan.

flow available with us.
