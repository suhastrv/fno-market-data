          9M revenue up 20% to ₹5151 cr; EBITDA up 11% to ₹1086 cr
          High double-digit commodity cost inflation addressed with pricing actions

Mumbai, 24 October 2026: Castrol India Limited (BSE: 500870; NSE: CASTROLIND) has
announced its results for the third quarter (3Q) and nine months (9m) ended 30 September
2026. The Company follows the calendar year (January to December) for its financial
reporting.

Key financial highlights:

Third quarter of 2026
    Revenue from Operations at ₹1735 Crore, growth of 27% (YoY)
    EBITDA dips 19% (YoY) to ₹262 crore

Nine months of 2026
    Revenue from Operations at ₹5151 Crore, up 20% (YoY)
    EBITDA up 11% (YoY) to ₹1086 Crore

Saugata Basuray, Managing Director, Castrol India Limited, said, “In a quarter
marked by a sharp rise in raw material costs and adverse currency movements, Castrol
delivered a resilient performance. Pricing actions taken early in the year helped moderate
the impact on margins, while we continued to invest in expanding our distribution network
in rural India and accelerating our industrial business, which delivered another quarter of
double-digit volume growth.”

Mrinalini Srinivasan, Chief Financial Officer, Castrol India Limited, said, “Our
EBITDA grew 11% over nine months, supported by pricing action we took early in the
year. As communicated in August, we expect the operating environment to remain volatile
and inflationary. We will act with agility to manage margin pressures through pricing and
cost actions, while remaining focused on growing market share across every segment in
which we operate,”

About Castrol India Limited
Castrol India Limited, part of the bp group, is a leading lubricant company with over 115 years presence in India. Known
