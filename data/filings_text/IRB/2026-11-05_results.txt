IRB Infra announces Q2FY27 Financial Results; Robust Toll Growth
        and Distribution from InvITs drives YoY PAT by 85%


Mumbai, November 5, 2026: India’s leading and the largest highway infrastructure assets development
and management platform, IRB Infrastructure Developers Limited, today announced its financial results
for Q2FY27, reporting a robust YoY PAT growth of around 85% to ₹260 Crs against Q2FY26, led by a strong
growth in distribution from InvITs.

Financial Highlights:

   •   Net Profit: ₹260Crs, against ₹141Crs in Q2FY26; YoY increase of 85%

   •   Total Income: ₹1,945Crs, against ₹1,800Crs in Q2FY26 driven by sturdy growth in toll collections

   •   EBITDA: ₹1,102Crs, against ₹974Crs in Q2FY26

Business Highlights:

   •   Company’s Private InvIT Joint Venture, IRB Infrastructure Trust completed transfer of its two BOT
       highway assets to IRB InvIT Fund, Public InvIT sponsored by the Company; thus, unlocking equity
       of ₹2,744Crs.

Management Commentary:

Commenting on the robust financial and operational performance for Q2FY27, Shri Virendra D.
Mhaiskar, Chairman and Managing Director of IRB Infrastructure Developers Ltd., said:

“Against the backdrop of global economic deceleration, supply chain fragmentation, and
geopolitical volatility, our Q2FY27 performance highlights the exceptional counter-cyclical
strength and structural resilience of our business model. Driven by a robust YoY Toll Revenue
growth of around 15%, our operations continue to benefit from inelastic traffic demand and
effective tariff-linking mechanisms – as is reflected in steady growth in cashflow and thus
distributions from InvITs.” Elaborating on the road ahead and the broader macroeconomic
alignment, Shri Mhaiskar added: “Our BEST strategy continues to reap benefits and release capital
for growth – as we successfully transferred two more mature assets from our Private InvIT to the IRB
InvIT Fund (Public InvIT) unlocking over ₹27Bn. This strategic capital recycling insulates our balance
sheet from global credit tightening and provides us opportunities to explore new sectoral
opportunities worth approximately ₹80Bn. Building upon the strong foundation of our ₹940Bn asset
base, we remain firmly on track to achieve our strategic milestone of a ₹1,400Bn asset base by 2030,
delivering sustainable, long-term value for all our stakeholders.”

About IRB Group:

IRB Group is India’s leading and largest integrated infrastructure assets platform in the roads and
highways sector, with a current portfolio of 27 revenue-generating highway assets aggregating to approx.
₹940 billion across 13 Indian states. With approximately 1.5 million vehicle crossings daily across its
assets, the Group accounts for nearly one-tenth of India’s total toll revenue.

Having successfully completed its asset creation phase, the Group has entered a cash-harvesting phase,
supported by its InvIT monetization strategy and sponsor-led operating leverage. With a weighted average
residual concession life of approximately 21 years, the portfolio provides strong visibility of long-term
cashflows through predictable toll revenues, recurring O&M income and growing InvIT distributions.

The Group has a clear roadmap to expand its asset base to approximately ₹1,400 billion by FY2030 with
asset churn strategy and reinvesting proceeds into new opportunities to drive sustainable growth and
long-term value creation.
