PRESS RELEASE



      Bandhan Bank Q2 FY27 PAT at Rs.622 cr, delivers 5-fold growth
  •   Gross advances grew 13% YoY to Rs.1.58 lakh crore as of September 2026
  •   Deposits grew 9% YoY reaching Rs.1.73 lakh crore as of September 2026
  •   Q2 FY27 GNPA at 3.1% vs 5% in Q2 FY26 and QFY27 net NPA at 0.9% vs 1.4% in Q2 FY26
  •   Banks Capital Adequacy Ratio stood at 18.3%.
The Board of Directors of Bandhan Bank Limited approved the Bank’s financial results for the
quarter and six months ended Sept 30, 2026, at its meeting held in Kolkata on Wednesday, October
28, 2026.

FINANCIAL RESULTS

Advances

As of Sept 30, 2026, Gross Advances stood at Rs.1.58 lakh crore as against Rs.1.40 lakh crore in the
previous year – a growth of 13% YoY

Secured Advances grew by 18% YoY and is now above 57% of the total advances as of Q2FY27 as
compared to 55% a year ago.

On a YoY basis, Retail book (other than housing) grew 42%, Wholesale Banking grew 32%, Housing
book showed a growth of 4%, while the EEB book registered a growth of 2% YoY.

Deposits

As of Sept 30, 2026, total deposits stood at Rs.1.73 lakh crore as against Rs.1.58 lakh crore in the
previous year – a growth of 9% YoY.

CASA Deposits stood at Rs.46,132 crore and CASA Ratio stood at 27%.

CASA + Retail Term Deposits to total deposit ratio stands at 73%.

Asset Quality

The collection efficiency for EEB loans was higher at 99.3% for Q2 FY27

The Bank has seen sequential improvement in the SMA 0, SMA 1 and SMA 2 buckets for EEB
portfolio.

The bank's Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA) stands flat at 3.1% in Q2 FY27, net NPA at 0.9%
same as in previous quarter.

Provision Coverage Ratio including write-offs as of Sept 30, 2026, is 86.7%.

Net interest income (NII) as of Q2 FY27, stood at Rs.2,960 crore compared to Rs.2,589 crore as of

The Bank’s net total income as of Q2 FY27 was Rs.3,665 crore as compared to Rs.3,135 crore in Q2

About Bandhan Bank:

Started as a universal bank on August 23, 2015, Bandhan Bank is one of India’s fastest-growing
private sector banks.

Bandhan Bank has always been committed to financial inclusion and aims to serve the underserved.
Guided by the principle of ‘Aapka Bhala, Sabki Bhalai,’ the Bank is dedicated not only to serving its
customers but also to making a positive difference in people’s lives. The Bank actively aims to bridge
the gap for those who may not have had easy access to conventional banking services. Its focus is
on providing a variety of world-class banking products and a comprehensive 360-degree service
proposition, including loans, deposit accounts, internet and mobile banking and a host of products
across India, primarily to semi-urban and rural customers.

In the last few years of operations, Bandhan Bank has spread its presence to 35 of the 36 states and
union territories in India with over 6400 banking outlets. With the trust of more than 3.2 crore
customers. Bandhan Bank has a deposit base of Rs.1.73 lakh crores as of Sept 30, 2026. Bandhan
Bank has achieved consistent growth and its advances stood at Rs.1.58 lakh crore as of Sept 30,
2026.
