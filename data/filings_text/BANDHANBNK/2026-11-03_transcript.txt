On the asset side, our loan book reached approximately INR1.58 lakh crores, representing a healthy 13%
YoY growth. Growth continued to be led by our non-EEB business, which expanded by 20% YoY, providing
greater diversification and multiple growth engines to the overall franchise.


And encouraging development this quarter has been the stabilization of the EEB portfolio. Following the
moderation witnessed in the last few quarters, the de-growth in EEB has now been arrested. While we
continue to remain prudent in this segment, the underlying trends have improved, and we are hopeful that
the portfolio should return to a healthy growth trajectory from here.


Our journey towards building a more secured and diversified balance sheet also continues to progress well.
The secured portfolio grew 18% YoY and now represents 57.3% of the total advances from 54.9% a year
ago. This gradual transformation of our portfolio mix is helping us build a more balanced risk profile while
retaining the core strengths of our franchise.


Housing is another business where we expect to see an improvement from here. Over the last few quarters,
we have undertaken significant changes in this business, including verticalization of the structure and several
other changes to strengthen execution. This transition impacted growth and resulted in some near-term asset
quality challenges. However, with most of these changes behind us, we believe the business is better
positioned for growth.


Turning to liability. The progress during the quarter has been particularly encouraging. Total deposits
increased to approximately INR 1.73 lakh crores with a strong 4.7% sequential growth, without any support
from the FCNR (B) deposits. Importantly, this helped bring our net credit-deposit ratio down to 89.5% from
around 92% in the previous quarter.


Retail term deposits continue to demonstrate healthy momentum, growing by around 17% YoY without any
significant contribution from FCNR deposit mobilization. This gives us greater confidence in the underlying
strength and sustainability of our retail deposit franchise.


At the same time, I'd also like to acknowledge that our performance in saving back account in the quarter
was below our expectations. We saw some balances move out following the recalibration of interest rates
across certain savings bank buckets.


While this affected balances in the near term, our approach is very clear. We want to build a granular and
sustainable savings account franchise, anchored around deeper customer relationships, transactions and
engagement, rather than being disproportionately dependent on rate sensitive deposits.


We believe this is the right approach for strengthening the quality of our liability franchise over the medium
term. To do so, we are working on several levers. Corporate salary accounts are an important opportunity
and our customer proposition is becoming broader with products such as credit cards.


We are also strengthening our proposition for HNI and ultra HNI customers through specific product
offerings. At the same time, our larger objective is to grow the mass banking customer base through granular
acquisition and deepen relationships with our existing asset customers through cross-sell and strengthening
digital platforms.

We also witnessed an increase in bulk deposits during the quarter. Let me clarify that this does not represent
any change in our liability strategy. A significant portion of this incremental bank deposits was raised at
shorter tenures primarily for liquidity management, while at the same time we reduced relatively higher cost
borrowings. Our philosophy on bulk deposits, therefore, remains unchanged. We do not intend to use bulk
funding as a structural source to drive loan growth. It will be used selectively for liquidity and balance sheet
management, while our primary focus continues to be on building granular retail deposits.


Coming to profitability. Our performance during the quarter demonstrates the underlying resilience of the
franchise. Despite changes in the deposit mix, increase in the secured advances mix and moderation in
savings bank account balances, NIMs remained broadly stable at 6.1%.


Our overall asset quality position remained healthy, with gross NPA at 3.1% and net NPA at 0.9%. Provision
coverage, including technical write-offs, remained strong at approximately 87%. Credit costs also remained
broadly stable sequentially at 1.8%.


The progress we are making across the franchisee is also becoming increasingly reasonable in our earnings
performance. Net total income for Q2 FY27 stood at INR 3,655 crores, growing 17% YoY. While operating
profit increased by a strong 19% YoY to INR 1,556 crores. Profit after tax for the quarter stood at INR 622
crores, which is 5.5x YoY.


More importantly, our return ratios showed meaningful improvement during the quarter with ROA, ROE
improving to 1.2% and 9.3%, respectively, versus 0.2% and 1.8% in Q2 FY26.


While there is still further ground to cover, the direction of movement gives us confidence that our efforts
across business growth, balance sheet quality and profitability are translating into better outcomes.


Our capital proposition continues to provide a strong foundation for our growth aspirations. Including profit,
our capital adequacy ratio stood at 18.3% with a Tire 1 capital at 17.7%, providing adequate headroom to
support future growth while maintaining a prudent capital buffer.


Our physical distribution network also expanded further as during the quarter, we added 13 new branches
and 14 banking units taking the total count to 2001 branches and 4,414 banking units.


Overall, I would characterize the second quarter as one of the balanced and steady progress quarter. Deposit
mobilization has gathered momentum, our net CD ratio has moderated, the EEB portfolio has stabilized,
diversification towards secured businesses continues, asset quality remains under control, and profitability
and return ratios have improved substantially.


As we enter the second half of the financial year, our priorities remain clear. We will continue to focus on
accelerating granular deposit growth, rebuilding momentum in saving accounts, growing our business in a
disciplined manner, maintaining asset quality and improving operating efficiency. We also expect the actions
taken across EEB and housing to increasingly contribute to the growth trajectory in the coming quarters.


While the external operating environment continues to require careful monitoring, we remain focused on the
factors within our control. Our objective is not merely to grow the balance sheet, but to improve the quality,
resilience and profitability of the franchise as we grow.
