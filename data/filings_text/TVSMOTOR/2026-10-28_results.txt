                     TVS Motor Achieves Highest ever Revenue
                          with 44% Growth in Q2 2026-27

       •   Record Revenue of Rs. 17,103 Crores in Q2 2026-27
       •   Highest Ever Sales of 1.9 Million Units
       •   Profit After Tax Grew by 52% at Rs. 1,377 Crores


Bengaluru, October 28, 2026: TVS Motor Company, part of TVS VENU, posted revenue
for the second quarter of financial year 2026-27 at Rs. 17,103 Crores, registering a growth of
44% as against Rs. 11,905 Crores recorded in the second quarter of 2025-26.

The Company posted its highest EBITDA of Rs. 2,219 Crores with a growth of 47% for the
second quarter of 2026-27 as against EBITDA of Rs. 1,507 Crores in the same period of
2025-26. The Company’s EBITDA margin improved by 30bps at 13.0% during the quarter
ended September 2026 as against 12.7% in the quarter ended September 2025.

During the current quarter, the Company’s Profit After Tax grew by 52% at Rs. 1,377 Crores
as against Rs. 904 Crores during the second quarter of 2025-26.

SALES Q2 FY 2026-27:

During the second quarter of the financial year 2026-27, the Company registered the highest
ever quarterly sales. The overall two and three-wheeler sales grew by 27% at 1.9 Million units
as against 1.5 Million units registered in the quarter ended September 2025. Motorcycles
sales during the quarter under review grew by 28% recording 0.9 Million units as against 0.7
Million units in the quarter ended September 2025. Scooters sales during the quarter under
review grew by 29% at 0.8 Million units as against 0.6 Million units in the quarter ended
September 2025. International Business reported a growth of 35% with sales of 0.5 Million
units in Q2 2026-27 as against 0.4 Million units in the second quarter of 2025-26.

Two-wheeler electric vehicles grew by 133% registering sales of 186,186 units in the second
quarter of 2026-27 as against 80,009 units during second quarter of 2025-26.

Three-wheelers sales for the quarter under review grew by 47% to 78,475 units in the second
quarter ended September 2026 as against 53,449 units in the quarter ended September
2025.

Half-year results:

The total two-wheeler sales of the Company for the half-year ended September 2026 grew
by 27% registering 3.4 Million units as against 2.7 Million units recorded in the half-year ended
September 2025. The total three-wheeler sales for the half-year ended September 2026 grew
by 47% at 145,172 units as against 98,427 units registered in the half-year ended September
2025. The total sales in International Business of two and three-wheelers for the half-year
ended September 2026 grew by 34% at 1.0 Million units as against 0.8 Million units in the
half-year ended September 2025.

   TVS MOTOR COMPANY ANNOUNCES DEMERGER OF
     CONSUMER FINANCE AND RELATED BUSINESS
                 UNDERTAKING

    •   Proposed demerger will unlock shareholder value through a focused platform for the
                         Group's consumer finance and related businesses
        • Provides greater strategic autonomy, sharper capital allocation and enhanced
                               flexibility to pursue sector opportunities
   •    Shareholders to participate in the future growth of both mobility and financial services
                                                businesses

Bengaluru, October 28, 2026: The Board of Directors of TVS Motor Company, part
of TVS VENU, today approved a Composite Scheme of Arrangement (Scheme) to
demerge its consumer finance and related business undertaking into a separate
holding company. Under the proposed Scheme, shareholders of the company will
receive equal number of shares in the resulting company as in TVS Motor, enabling
them to participate in the future growth of both the mobility and consumer finance
businesses.

The consumer finance and related business have expanded in scale and scope and
a dedicated structure will provide greater strategic focus, flexibility in capital
allocation and the ability to pursue opportunities across the consumer finance and
related sectors. The proposed structure will better align shareholders with the future
growth of these businesses while supporting their continued expansion.

In August 2026, the NBFC Companies within the Group have approved a proposal to
merge and consolidate the Group’s consumer finance businesses into a single
operating platform. The proposed demerger is the next step in this strategic process
and is intended to segregate the Consumer finance and related businesses from
TVS Motor into a dedicated platform and reflects the Group's long-term conviction in
India's financial services sector and its role in supporting economic growth, financial
inclusion and rising aspirations across the country.
The proposed structure will also enable TVS Motor to sharpen its focus on its
continuing automotive and mobility businesses. The Group remains committed to the
growth and development of both the dedicated consumer finance platform and TVS
Motor’s continuing business.

Sudarshan Venu, Chairman, TVS Motor Company, said: "India's rapidly
expanding financial services sector offers tremendous opportunities for long-term
growth. Driven by digitisation, expanding credit access and the aspirations of India's
growing middle class, financial services are playing an increasingly important role in
one of the world's fastest-growing large economies. At TVS VENU, we have built
meaningful presence and capabilities in this sector through sustained investments
over 15 years. Our NBFC TVS Credit, along with Home Credit India, serves more

The businesses being demerged have developed strong capabilities, customer
