Before we start the official business of the AGM, I would like to talk briefly about the progress of
the Company has made during the year that went by.

As we presented the Integrated Annual Report for FY 2026, Zydus Wellness stands at the
intersection of evolving lifestyles, changing aspirations, and growing desire to live better and
healthy life every day. The theme “Choose Wellness, Choose Life” captures a powerful truth.
Wellness is shaped by the choices we make every single day. Across the world, consumers are
becoming more conscious of how they live, what they consume and how they care for their
health and well-being. Wellness is no longer viewed as an occasional choice; it is increasingly
becoming a way of life. At Zydus Wellness, we believe this shift presents both an opportunity and

responsibility. An opportunity to serve every consumer’s need and a responsibility to do so with
trust, science and integrity.

FY26 was a year that tested resilience while creating new avenues for growth. Consumer demand
patterns continue to evolve, weather conditions impacted certain seasonal categories and
businesses across industries had to navigate an increasingly dynamic environment. Yet despite
these challenges, we remained focus on our long-term priorities and continue to strengthen the
foundation of our business. Our performance during the year reflects the strength of our
portfolio, the commitment of our teams and the disciplined execution of our strategy. Revenue
from operations increased by 46.2% to Rs. 36,610 million. While EBITDA grew 34.2% to Rs. 5,097
million. While certain parts of our portfolio faced temporary challenges, the overall business
remained resilient. Within our domestic portfolio, seasonal brands were impacted by delayed
summers and unseasonal rainfall. At the same time, our skincare haircare portfolio delivered
strong growth supported by innovation, premiumisation and deeper consumer engagements.

Our food and nutrition segment witnessed continued growth reflecting consumers increasing
focus towards healthier lifestyles and the strength of our offerings in these categories. The
Wellness landscape continued to expand rapidly. Consumers today seek products that support
healthier living and preventive care and better nutrition, while expecting greater convenience
accessibility and authenticity. We are responding by strengthening our portfolio – investing in
innovation, building deeper consumer connections across both the physical and digital channels.

Our portfolio comprises iconic brands such as Sugar Free, Glucon-D, Complan, Everyuth, Nycil,
Nutralite, RiteBite Max Protein, and I'm Lite, which continues to enjoy the trust of millions of
consumers built over years through consistent quality, reliability and science-led innovation,
these brands continue to evolve through new formats, product innovations and wellness-
oriented positioning. By responding to emerging trends such as functional nutrition,
convenience-led consumption and healthier-conscious choices, we continue to strengthen their
relevance while sustaining category leadership.

A significant milestone during the year was the acquisition of Comfort Click Limited in the United
Kingdom, along with its three subsidiaries in Ireland, India and the United States. This strategic

step represents much more than the addition of a business. It strengthens our participation in
the fast-growing Vitamins, Minerals and Supplements category, expands our presence across key
international markets, and enhances our capabilities in digital-first and direct-to-consumer
segments.

Our international business, including Comfort Click, delivered strong double-digit growth,
reflecting the increasing relevance of our brands and platforms across the global markets. With
more than 30% of our revenue generated from international business, we have evolved into a
truly global and digital company and further have been able to reduce the seasonal impact on
our business.

Today, we are strategically aligned with emerging global health and wellness trends through a
diversified portfolio spanning low-sugar products, high-protein nutrition, hydration and energy
solutions, functional skin and hair care, active lifestyle products, and vitamins, minerals and
supplements.

As we continue to grow, innovation remains at the heart of our journey. Our focus is not simply
on introducing new products, but also developing solutions that are relevant, meaningful and
backed by consumer insight and scientific understanding. Whether through nutrition, hydration,
personal care or preventive wellness, our main objective remains the same—helping consumers
make healthier choices every day.

We also remain committed to creating long-term value responsibly. I am pleased that Zydus
Wellness has been recognised in the S&P Global Sustainability Yearbook 2026, ranking amongst
the leading performers in our sector. This recognition reflects the steady progress we continue
to make in embedding sustainability into our business practices and our long-term strategy.

None of our achievements would have been possible without our people. Their dedication,
resilience and belief in our purpose to continue to drive our success. I am particularly pleased
that we have once again have been recognised as a Great Place to Work for the fourth
consecutive year and as we speak the Company has been recognized among India's Best

Workplaces™ in FMCG 2026. This recognition reflects the culture of trust, collaboration and
shared ambition that defines our organisation.

As I look ahead, I remain optimistic about the future. Rising awareness across preventive
healthcare, nutrition and overall well-being continues to create significant opportunities for
businesses that combine innovation with trust. With our strong brands, expanding global
footprint, growing digital capabilities and diversified wellness portfolio, we are well positioned
to capture these opportunities.

We continue to strengthen our brands, expand our global wellness platforms, invest in
innovation, deepen consumer engagement and pursue sustainable, profitable growth. At every
stage, we will remain guided by our purpose and our commitment to create long-term value for
all our shareholders.

Thank you for your continued trust and support. Together, we will continue to build a stronger
organisation, create lasting value and remain true to our purpose of helping people who choose
wellness and choose life.

Wishing you and your families good health, happiness and well-being.
