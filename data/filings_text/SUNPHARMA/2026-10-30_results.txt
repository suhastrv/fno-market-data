Mumbai, October 30, 2026: Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Limited (Reuters: SUN.BO, Bloomberg: SUNP IN,
NSE: SUNPHARMA, BSE: 524715) reported financials for the second quarter ending September 30th, 2026.
Highlights of Q2FY27 consolidated financials*

•      Sales at Rs. 161,000 million, a growth of 11.8%.
•      Global Innovative Medicines sales at US$ 356 million, up 6.9% and account for 21.1% of sales.
•      EBITDA at Rs. 47,439 million, up 4.8%. EBITDA margin at 29.3%.
•      Profit before exceptional items and tax was Rs 44,282 million, up 6.3%.
•      Net profit was Rs. 19,885 million, adjusted net profit was Rs. 31,620 million.
•      R&D investment was Rs. 7,784 million at 4.8% of sales.

Highlights of H1FY27 consolidated financials*

•      Sales at Rs. 312,836 million, a growth of 11.0%.
•      Global Innovative Medicines sales stood at US$ 707 million, up 9.8% and account for 21.5% of sales.
•      EBITDA was Rs. 91,616 million, up 3.8%. EBITDA margin was 29.1%.
•      Profit before exceptional items and tax was Rs 87,311 million, up 7.0%.
•      Net profit was Rs. 48,833 million, adjusted net profit was Rs. 62,515 million, up 2.2%.
•      R&D investment was Rs. 16,048 million at 5.1% of sales.
*Growth measured vs same period last financial year

Kirti Ganorkar, Managing Director of the Company, said, “Our performance during the quarter was driven by
strong momentum in India, Emerging Markets and Rest of World. Among key developments, Ilumya is now
approved in the U.S. for the treatment of adults with active psoriatic arthritis. Unloxcyt’s preferred NCCN
recommendation reinforces its durable response and good tolerability in aCSCC. Beyond dermatology, we
strengthened our cardiovascular portfolio through the licensing of lerodalcibep, a PCSK9 inhibitor, for markets
outside US and China. We have started pre-launch activities across EU.”

Organon Acquisition Update

Integration planning for the Organon acquisition continues to progress across functions and workstreams, in line
with plan. Regulatory approvals have now been secured in majority of regions, with the remaining
jurisdictions tracking on schedule.

India Formulations

Formulation sales in India were Rs. 55,523 million, up by 17.3% and accounting for 34.5% of total consolidated
sales for the quarter. For the first half of the year, sales were Rs. 110,272 million, growing by 16.6%.

Sun Pharma continues to be India’s No. 1 Pharma company, with market share increasing to 8.6% from 8.3%, as
per the Pharmarack MAT Sept-2026 report. As per SMSRC (Jul-Sept 2026) report, Sun Pharma is ranked No. 1
by prescriptions in 12 doctor categories. The company launched 14 new products during the quarter and 19
products year to date.

US Formulations

Formulation sales in the US were US$ 455 million for Q2FY27, registering a decline of 8.2%. US sales accounted
for approximately 27% of total consolidated sales. For the first half, sales were US$ 882 million.

Ilumya is now approved in the U.S. for the treatment of adults with active psoriatic arthritis. As a healthcare
provider-administered IL-23 inhibitor, Ilumya offers an additional treatment option for adults with active
psoriatic arthritis, particularly those who are unable to self-administer therapy or who may benefit from regular
in-office care. The approval was supported by two 52-week, global, multicenter, randomized, double-blind,
placebo-controlled Phase 3 studies evaluating the efficacy and safety of the product.

Emerging Markets (EM) Formulations

Emerging Markets Formulations sales were US$ 350 million for Q2FY27, up by 7.6% and accounted for 20.7%
of total consolidated sales. For the first half, sales were US$ 661 million, growing by 6.0%.

Rest of World (ROW) Formulations

Formulation sales in Rest of World (RoW) markets were US$ 241 million for Q2FY27, up by 3.1% and
accounted for 14.3% of total consolidated sales for the quarter. For the first half, sales were US$ 460 million, up
by 1.5%.

Innovative Medicines

Global Innovative Medicines sales were US$ 356 million for Q2FY27, up 6.9% and accounted for 21.1% of total
consolidated sales for the quarter. For the first half, sales were US$ 707 million, up by 9.8%.

Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (API)

For Q2FY27, external sales of API were Rs. 4,889 million, up by 13.7%. For the first half, API sales were Rs.
10,861 million, up by 11.9%. Our API portfolio supports our formulation business and API customers across
geographies.

R&D Update

Sun Pharma’s R&D efforts span across both innovative and generic businesses and the company continues to
invest in building the pipeline for various markets. Sun’s innovative R&D pipeline includes five novel entities in
clinical stage. Sun has a comprehensive product offering in the US market consisting of approved ANDAs for 561
products while filings for 120 ANDAs await US FDA approval, including 26 tentative approvals. During the
quarter, 4 ANDAs were filed, and approval received for 3 ANDAs. Sun’s portfolio includes 58 approved NDAs
while 12 NDAs await US FDA approval.
