Escalating geopolitical tensions in the Middle East triggered significant crude oil volatility, driving
raw material cost inflation across key chemical value chains. Almost all of our raw materials saw
significant price increase, while RMs such as Sulphur saw prices surge by over 5-6x, combined with
sharply elevated freight costs, strained global supply chain economics brought out challenges of the
sort of soaring working capital and debt levels, maintaining supply chain stability to cater to market
demands, etc.


We believe the shifting dynamics in global trade present a compelling tailwind for Indian specialty
chemical manufacturers, including Aarti Industries. China’s ongoing capacity rationalization, paired
with India’s expanding trade agreements with countries like the UK and Europe, is set to solidify
India’s position in global supply chains. With our deeply integrated manufacturing footprint and long-
standing client relationships, we are well-positioned to capitalize on this shift — a trend that is
beginning to translate into volume growth and margin expansion across our key value chains.


Against this structural backdrop, our quarterly performance across key end-user segments reflected
varying sector dynamics. Let me take you through it, segment by segment.


●         Dyes & Pigments: Despite inflationary prices pressures from key raw materials like
Sulphur, we delivered steady growth anchored by resilient domestic demand for NCB chain.


●         Agrochemicals: While demand across select categories within the Agchem chain remains
stable, allowing us to achieve volume growth, pricing trends remain mixed. The second half might
reflect the impact of weaker monsoon in the domestic market.


●         Pharma & Fluoro Products: We achieved pricing and volume gains in PNCB. Key
paracetamol building blocks saw robust demand, bolstered by capacity rationalizations in China. The
Fluoro products’ margins continue to remain under pressure.


●         Polymers & Additives: Demand dynamics across high-end engineering, EV, and defense
markets remain robust. While softness in the PDA chain created a short-term drag on our Q2 FY27
performance, we expect traction to resume in Q3 FY27, underpinned by accelerating DCB demand.


●         Energy-Linked Portfolio: Blending dynamics remain favourable on the back of elevated
crude oil prices and strong gasoline-naphtha cracks. Performance was also supported by price increase
undertaken due to higher RM costs. To build a more steady and sustained business, we are
transitioning towards a solutions-based approach across the broader Energy value chain. Leveraging
our bulk shipment capabilities and robust R&D pipeline, we anticipate launching of several new
solutions over the next 12 to 24 months.

under review.
