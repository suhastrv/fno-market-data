       effective from 22 August 2026, inserted Section 9D in the MMDR Act, 1957, restricting States

       accordance with conditions prescribed by the Central Government. The prescribed

       ~16, 188.56 crore, representing the potential exposure under the proposed Bill, subject to the
       outcome of pending proceedings and future developments.

       imposition of statutory duties, levies or taxes is contractually recoverable from

       Steel Limited. As per the terms, NMDC shall act in trust for NSL and accordingly, an amount of
       ~ 2,502.64 crores was incurred by NMDC on behalf of NSL with no specific repayment schedule
       defined in the scheme. Against this amount, NSL has repaid an amount of~ 1001.25 crore and
       the balance outstanding receivable as on September 30, 2026 amounts to ~ 1,501.39 crores.
       Based on the estimate of recoverability, an amount of ~69.98 crore is recognised as Expected
       Credit Loss (ECL) provision.

         • ~ 4578.38 crores towards sale of iron ore (trade receivables),
         • ~ 162.26 crores for employee-related services, and

       Company has received an amount of ~1,833 . 09 crore against the trade receivable from NSL
       under a bill discounting arrangement which is on recourse to the Company. Hence, the said
       amounts received are recognised as borrowings under Current Financial Liabilities.
