Financial highlights

Revenue for the quarter was Rs. 11,884 Cr, a YoY growth of 6.2% and 1.7% on a QoQ basis. Cash
EBITDA for the quarter was Rs. 2,498 Cr, a YoY growth of 11.2%. EBITDA for the quarter was Rs. 4,981
Cr, a YoY growth of 6.3%.

Loss adjusted for exceptional items during the quarter was lower at Rs. 5,513 Cr compared to Rs.
5,562 Cr for Q2FY26. Reported loss for the quarter was higher at Rs. 5,975 Cr due to adverse impact
of mark-to-market of treasury assets.

Capex for the quarter stood at Rs. 3,946 Cr. As on September 30, 2026, the bank debt stood at Rs.
943 Cr while the cash and bank balance stood at Rs. 5,470 Cr.

We have secured a total funding of over Rs. 10,500 Cr till H1FY27 including warrants, fund & non-
fund based facilities of which over Rs. 4,000 Cr of incremental bank funding was arranged in Q2FY27.
Capex orders worth over Rs. 13,200 Cr have been placed towards our three-year capex guidance.

Operational highlights

We have expanded our 5G coverage to 400 Indian cities and towns across the 17 key circles where
we hold 5G spectrum.

Alongside 5G rollout, we continue to invest in expanding our high-speed broadband network by
adding new 4G sites and upgrading our core and transmission network. We added over 22,500 new
unique broadband towers over the last twelve months, and our total unique broadband towers count
now stands at nearly 214,000. During the second quarter alone, we added nearly 9,000 towers, which
is the highest quarterly addition in the last nine quarters. Our 4G population coverage increased to
almost 88% as of September 2026. We have extended our 4G coverage to an additional 45 million
people since Q2FY26. The momentum of our network rollout reinforces our focus on delivering
superior customer experience through enhanced indoor coverage, while also adding more sites to
expand our capacity. With our planned investments, the 4G population coverage in the 17 key circles
is expected to increase to over 95%.

Our Customer ARPU increased to Rs. 198 in Q2FY27 from Rs. 180 in Q2FY26, a YoY increase of 9.7%,
primarily supported by customer upgrades. Our total subscriber base stood at 194.4 million
compared to 193.1 million in Q1FY27. We closed the quarter with 131.0 million 4G/5G subscribers,
up from 127.8 million in the same period last year. The data usage improved from 73.6 PB/Day to
93.6 PB/Day, a YoY increase of 27.1%.

About Vodafone Idea Ltd.

Vodafone Idea Limited is an Aditya Birla Group and Vodafone Group partnership. It is amongst India’s leading telecom service providers. The company
holds a large spectrum portfolio including mid band 5G spectrum in 17 circles and mmWave spectrum in 16 circles. The Company provides Voice and
Data services across 2G, 4G and 5G platforms and is expanding 5G services across 17 circles. To support the growing demand for data and voice, the
Company is committed to delivering delightful customer experiences and contributing towards creating a truly ‘Digital India’ by enabling millions of
citizens to connect and build a better tomorrow. The Company is developing infrastructure to introduce newer and smarter technologies, making both
retail and enterprise customers future ready with innovative offerings, conveniently accessible through an ecosystem of digital channels as well as
extensive on-ground presence. The Company’s equity shares are listed on National Stock Exchange (NSE) and the BSE in India.
