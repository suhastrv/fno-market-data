          HPCL profit falls 6% to
          ₹3,625 cr in second quarter
          Our Bureau
          New Delhi


          State-run Hindustan Petroleum Corporation (HPCL)
          on Thursday reported a 6 per cent y-o-y decline in its
          consolidated net profit at around ₹ 3,625 crore during
          Q2 FY27 as the gains from higher refining margins
          were offset by losses from the sale of petrol, diesel and
          liquefied petroleum gas (LPG).
             The PSU oil marketing company (OMC) had in-
          curred a loss of around ₹ 12,265 crore on a sequential
          basis.
             The OMC, which operates more than 25,000 fuel re-
          tail outlets nationwide, posted a consolidated total in-
          come of roughly ₹1.4 lakh crore in Q2 FY27, compared
          to ₹1.46 lakh crore in Q1 FY27 and ₹1.11 lakh crore in
          Q2 FY26.
             It reported consolidated total expenses of ₹1.33
          lakh crore in Q2 FY27 against ₹1.64 lakh crore in Q1
          FY27 and ₹1.06 lakh crore in Q2 FY26.

          ROBUST PERFORMANCE
          HPCL said the results reflect resilient and robust per-
          formance by the refineries and the marketing divi-
          sions of the company in the midst of the ongoing West
          Asia crisis. Accruals under Project Samridhi 2.0 (the
          EBITDA improvement programme) stood at ₹327
          crore, or around $0.18 per barrel, during H1 FY27.
             The company’s average gross refining margin
          (GRM) during April-September in FY27 was $27.89
          per barrel as against $5.95 a year ago. This is before
          factoring-in the impact of special additional excise
          duty and road & infrastructure cess on export of select
          petroleum products, it said in its results filing on the
          BSE.
             During April-September 2026 due to suppressed
          marketing margins on certain petroleum products,
          the profitability was impacted, it added.
             HPCL told BSE that the cumulative negative buffer
          (unrecognised) on sale of LPG cylinders below market
          rate on September 30, 2026, is around ₹18,048.82
          crore (March 31, 2026: around ₹12,798.67 crore and
          around ₹14,240.64 crore on September 30, 2025).
