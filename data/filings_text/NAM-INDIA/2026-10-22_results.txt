Mumbai, October 22, 2026: Nippon Life India Asset Management, asset manager of Nippon India
Mutual Fund (NIMF), announced its financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Mr. Sundeep Sikka, MD & CEO, NAM India, said “We continued our strong growth, with the highest
AUM growth and market share increase among the Top-10 AMCs in H1 FY27. Net Sales market
share and SIP market share both remain well above Equity AUM market share. We remain humbled
to have the trust of over 1 in every 3 mutual fund investors.”


Business Highlights

    •   NAM India’s Closing AUM grew 14% YoY to INR 8.68 trn (US$ 90.6 bn).

    •   NIMF’s MF QAAUM grew 20% YoY to INR 7.87 trn (US$ 82.1 bn).
    •   Equity MF QAAUM grew 20% YoY to INR 3.76 trn (US$ 39.2 bn), market share +27 bps YoY.
    •   ETF QAAUM grew 36% YoY to INR 2.49 trn (US$ 25.9 bn), market share +135 bps YoY.

    •   Retail MAAUM grew 18% YoY to INR 2.27 trn (US$ 23.7 bn), market share +38 bps YoY.
    •   High Networth Individual (HNI) MAAUM grew 28% YoY to INR 2.61 trn (US$ 27.2 bn) market
        share +105 bps YoY.

    •   Corporate AUM grew 12% YoY to INR 3.01 trn (US$ 31.4 bn).
    •   AUM from ‘Beyond the Top 30 cities’ (B-30) grew 23% YoY to INR 1.63 trn (US$ 17.0 bn)
        This forms 20.7% of NIMF’s AUM vs. 18.8% for the Industry.

    •   NIMF’s quarterly systematic flows rose by 4% YoY to INR 111.5 bn (US$ 1.2 bn). This
        resulted in an annualised systematic book of ~INR 445 bn (US$ 4.6 bn).
    •   Nippon India AIF offers Category II and Category III Alternative Investment Funds and has a
        total commitment of INR 99.8 bn (US$ 1.0 bn) across various schemes (up 14% YoY).

    •   NIMF has a pan-India presence at 271 locations - amongst the highest in the industry.
    •   Digital purchase transactions rose to 9.00 mn in H1 FY27, up 15% YoY. Digital channel
        contributed 78% to new purchase transactions in H1 FY27.

Financial Highlights – Q2 FY27

    •   Total Income stood at INR 8.19 bn (US$ 85 mn), up 18% YoY.

    •   Operating Profit stood at INR 5.23 bn (US$ 55 mn), up 25% YoY.

    •   Profit after Tax stood at INR 3.24 bn (US$ 34 mn), down 6% YoY.
