production capacity. The back-to-school category delivered strong growth, led by demand of

improved diaper sales driven by favorable climatic conditions. This performance reflects the

Overall, while we continue to operate in a volatile cost environment, our focus remains on

Coming to our financial performance for Q2 and HY FY27. Operating revenue for Q2 FY27
grew by 17.6% to INR667.8 crores, while H1 FY27 revenue grew by 18.4%. While the quarter
demonstrated underlying business momentum, we also faced an exceptional challenge in the
form of unusually heavy rainfall and flooding in Gujarat, which led to inventory damages and
workforce mobility challenges impacting production and sales. We estimate an impact of
approximately 300 to 350 basis points on our sales growth during Q2 FY27 due to this.


EBITDA for Q2 FY27 stood at INR82.8 crores and H1 FY27 EBITDA stood at INR165.3 crores
with margins during both the periods at 12.4%. Despite continued volatility in raw material
prices, we maintained our EBITDA margin sequentially during the quarter. While raw material
prices softened in the earlier part of the quarter, they increased significantly in the second half.
The improvement in consumption margins supported by initial easing in raw material prices and
our marginal price increases was offset by higher manpower costs. As part of the Reynolds asset
purchase agreement, the entire Reynolds sales and support teams joined DOMS effective July
1. While the associated employee costs were incurred from that date, revenue contribution from
the acquired assets remained limited during the quarter as these assets were being relocated to
DOMS in Umbergaon. This temporary mismatch between employee cost and correspondent
revenue contribution weighed on our profitability. As operations scale up and sales from these
assets increase, we expect this impact to normalize over time.


PAT for Q2 FY27 stood at INR38.3 crores with margins at 5.7%. H1 FY27 PAT stood at
INR83.6 crores with margins at 6.2%. At the PAT level, the quarter includes a onetime
exceptional loss of INR10.4 crores arising from damage caused by heavy rainfall and flooding.
We have already launched the relevant insurance claims and the associated recoveries is
expected to substantially offset the financial impact in due course.


On the working capital front, despite strong volume-led growth and a challenging market
environment, we have maintained our working capital cycle within the guided range of 55 to 60
days. Our focus remains on the quality of growth supported by financial discipline and healthy
cash conversion. We are pursuing measured, sustainable growth rather than pushing sales
through extended credit terms or allowing receivables to build up. At the same time, we continue
to closely manage inventory levels, which have marginally increased due to higher carrying
costs. Further, we continue to maintain discipline in payables, ensuring that our growth is
supported by efficient working capital management.
