Credit growth

Total advances increased by 30.0% year-on-year and 12.3% sequentially to ₹ 18,31,580
crore (US$ 191.1 billion) at September 30, 2026. Excluding loans provided by
international branches of the Bank against FCNR (B) deposits, total loans grew by 24.1%
year-on-year and 7.3% sequentially. The retail and rural loan portfolio grew by 14.8%
year-on-year and 3.2% sequentially, and comprised 51.6% of the total loan portfolio at
September 30, 2026. Including non-fund outstanding, the retail and rural portfolio was
43.0% of the total portfolio at September 30, 2026. The business banking portfolio grew
by 33.8% year-on-year and 11.1% sequentially at September 30, 2026. The domestic
