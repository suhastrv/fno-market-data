                                                                     Company Overview
      Deepak Fertilisers and Petrochemicals Corporation Ltd. (DFPCL) is one of India’s foremost producers of industrial chemicals and crop
      nutrition solutions, driving progress across agriculture, pharmaceuticals, mining, manufacturing, and infrastructure for over four
      decades. Built on a foundation of operational excellence, innovation, and customer-centricity, DFPCL has emerged as a trusted partner
      in India’s growth journey.
      With strategically located manufacturing complexes in Maharashtra, Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, and Haryana, the company is uniquely
      positioned to serve the evolving needs of India’s industrial and agricultural sectors at scale.
      As India advances toward greater self-reliance and sustainable development, DFPCL is transforming its portfolio through differentiated
      products, advanced technologies, and value-added solutions that enhance productivity, efficiency, and resource stewardship.
      DFPCL is India’s largest producer of Nitric Acid and the country’s leading manufacturer and marketer of Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA),
      supplying critical inputs to a broad range of industries. The company continues to expand its portfolio with specialized grades and
      innovative solutions designed to meet the increasingly sophisticated requirements of domestic and global markets.

      Its wholly owned subsidiary, Mahadhan AgriTech Limited (MAL), is redefining crop nutrition through science-led, differentiated
      offerings including Croptek, Smartek, and Solutek. Customized for specific crops and growth stages, these solutions are backed by
      extensive R&D and validation across more than 95,000 demonstration plots, consistently delivering higher yields, improved crop
      quality, and enhanced farmer prosperity.
      Deepak Mining Solutions Limited (DMSL), another wholly owned subsidiary, is India’s leading producer of Technical Ammonium
      Nitrate and is expanding its capabilities with world-class technical services that help improve mining productivity, operational
      efficiency, and safety.
      With a diversified multi-product portfolio spanning critical sectors of the economy, DFPCL creates value far beyond its products,
      strengthening industries, empowering farmers, enabling infrastructure growth, and building the foundations of a more resilient and
      sustainable India.
