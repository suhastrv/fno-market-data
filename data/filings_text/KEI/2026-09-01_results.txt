Subject: Clarification regarding Standalone and Consolidated Financial Results

Dear Sir/Madam,

With reference to the observation regarding the identical figures reported in the Standalone and
Consolidated Financial Results and the presentation thereof under Schedule III to the Companies Act,
2013, we wish to clarify as under:

The Company has only one Associate Company, KEI Cables SA (Pty) Limited, and no subsidiary or
joint venture. The Consolidated Financial Statements are prepared in accordance with the applicable
provisions of Ind AS, including the equity method for accounting of the Associate Company.

The Associate Company has accumulated losses, and the Company's investment therein has already
been fully impaired in accordance with Ind AS 36 – Impairment of Assets. Accordingly, no further share
of losses of the Associate Company is recognised in the Consolidated Financial Statements.

Consequently, there is no further impact arising from the Associate Company on the Consolidated
Financial Results, and hence the figures reported in the Standalone and Consolidated Financial Results
are identical.

The identical figures are therefore a consequence of the applicable accounting treatment and do not
arise due to any error or omission in the consolidation process.

We request the Exchange to kindly take the above clarification on record.
