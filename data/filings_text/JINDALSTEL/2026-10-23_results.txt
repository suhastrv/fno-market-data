Production for the quarter remained stable at 2.40 MT sequentially despite the
monsoon season. Sales volume increased 6% QoQ to 2.38 MT, supported by inventory
liquidation. The share of flats increased to 61% during the quarter compared to 53% in
Q1FY27.

Gross revenue increased 9% QoQ to ₹19,448 crore, driven by higher sales volume and
blended realization. Average Selling Price improved sequentially on a higher share of
flats in the overall sales mix and lower TMT sales within the longs. Domestic sales
accounted for 92% of total sales in Q2FY27, compared with 91% in Q1FY27. Adjusted
EBITDA stood at ₹2,504 crore, while PAT was ₹771 crore for the quarter.

Operational Update

During the quarter, Jindal Steel commissioned the 192 km Barbil–Angul slurry
pipeline, supported by India’s largest iron ore slurry-making and pumping complex
with a capacity of 18 MTPA. The integrated system enables efficient transportation of
iron ore from Barbil to Angul, strengthening raw-material logistics while reducing road
congestion, fuel consumption and associated emissions.

The Company was awarded a Guinness World Record for the world’s longest road
built exclusively with processed steel slag. The 4-lane, 1.85 km road, developed in
collaboration with CSIR–CRRI at Raigarh, demonstrates the productive use of steel slag
in road infrastructure and advances the Company’s circularity agenda.

Jindal Steel also received the World Steel Association’s 2026 Steelie Award for
Excellence in People for “Dakshata: Engineering safety, one duty position at a time.”

The company entered into a strategic partnership with IIT Hyderabad to advance
research, testing and development of high-strength, value-added steel solutions,
supporting product innovation and enhanced customer applications.
