          SpaceX windfall
          leads Ontario
          Teachers’ to 9.5%
          first-half gain
          Bloomberg

          Ontario Teachers’ Pension
          Plan earned 9.5 per cent in
          the first half of the year,
          driven by the initial public
          offering of Elon Musk’s
          SpaceX.
             Investments in venture
          growth, public equities and
          inflation-sensitive assets
          boosted returns, Chief Ex-
          ecutive Officer Jo Taylor
          said in a statement Monday.
             Net assets climbed to
          C$303.2 billion ($217.5
          billion).
             Ontario Teachers’ put
          money into Musk’s Space
          Exploration Technologies
          Corp in 2019, the inaugural
          investment for its venture
          growth arm.
             The space company now
          has a market capitalisation
          of $1.8 trillion after com-
          pleting its record-smashing
          IPO in June.
             That was a “significant
          contributor” to the pension
          fund’s strong first-half re-
          turn, Ontario Teachers’
          said. Venture growth com-
          panies now represent 9 per
          cent of its assets, up from 6
          per cent at the end of last
          year.

          PE STRATEGY
          The Toronto-based fund has
          revamped its private equity
          strategy after posting a loss
          in that asset class in 2025 for
          the first time in 16 years.
             Private equity comprises
          16 per cent of the entire
          portfolio, down three per-
          centage points from year-
          end, while public stocks are
          21 per cent of holdings, up
          from 18 per cent.
             Some of Ontario Teach-
          ers’ notable investments
          during the first half include
          the acquisition of four mul-
          tifamily residential assets in
          Germany alongside DW Ef-
          fectum Residential.
             The pension plan also
          participated in financing the
          spin out of UK energy tech-
          nology platform Kraken
          from Octopus Energy
          Group.
