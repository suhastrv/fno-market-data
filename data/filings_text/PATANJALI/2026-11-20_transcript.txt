Turning to segmental performance, the edible oil segment recorded its highest-ever quarterly

The segment runs on three engines: oil palm plantation, oil seeds crush, and branded oils, and
all three continue to perform well. In branded oils, soybean, rice bran, sunflower, and groundnut
oils did well, while mustard was seasonally softer as we are in its off-season. The global
vegetable oil market remained firm through the quarter, driven by tighter palm oil supplies and
strong biofuel demand. The USDA cut Indonesia's 26 - 27 palm oil production quite substantially
earlier this month, pointing to prolonged dryness, and lowered its export forecast to 21.6 million
tons.


Indonesia's B50 biodiesel mandate, in force since 1st July, is absorbing more palm oil
domestically, while soybean oil use in U.S. biofuels has been setting new monthly records.
Domestically, palm, soya, and sunflower oil are traded well above last year's levels through the
quarter. Sunflower oil eased in September after peaking in August, while palm and soya firmed
towards the end of the quarter.


Looking ahead, the higher global soybean crush and improved rapeseed and sunflower seed
production should support oilseed availability, while palm oil supply faces seasonal and
weather-related constraints in the coming months. India's import mix will continue to follow
relative prices and availability across oils.


During the quarter, sunflower oil supply chains also faced disruption, and our physical-oriented
hedging strategy and timely import purchases helped us navigate it and manage the price
volatility.


Very importantly, on 24th September, the government reduced the effective import duty on
crude palm oil and crude soybean oil from 16.5% to 11%, and on crude sunflower oil from
16.5% to 5.5%. Palm oil makes up almost 50% of our edible oil portfolio, and with the balance
in soya and sunflower. In line with our pass-through model, we are passing the benefit on to
consumers, which should support affordability and consumption in the coming months.


We also welcome the standardization of edible oil pack sizes to be implemented with a three-
month transition period, which will improve transparency and create a more level playing field
for organized national brands such as ours. Our oil palm plantation business generated revenue
