      In Q2FY27, Consolidated Revenue from Operations grew 13% YoY to ₹3,897 crore. The India business delivered 10%
      underlying volume growth, marking another quarter of robust volume growth. The International business sustained
      healthy momentum, recording 13% constant currency growth (CCG).
      The India business continued its strong performance, with revenue up 10% YoY to ₹2,916 crore, driven by 10% underlying
      volume growth, reflecting the strength of the core franchises and the continued scale-up of new businesses. Business
      fundamentals remained healthy, supported by strong offtakes and broad-based gains in market share and penetration.
      Over 95+% of the business gained or sustained market share and penetration on a MAT basis. Traditional and Organized
      Trade grew in double digits, alongside accelerated growth in Quick Commerce.
      Gross margin expanded ~600 bps YoY, supported by moderation in the copra prices and a favourable portfolio mix. A&P
      investments were up 44% YoY as we continued to invest behind strengthening the long-term equity of our brands and
      supporting growth initiatives. Backed by the strong expansion in gross margin, EBITDA margin expanded ~200 bps to 18.2%,
      while EBITDA grew 27%, its strongest growth in 50 quarters. PAT grew 31%, marking the highest growth in the last 51
      quarters.
      Key performance highlights for the quarter:
      • Parachute Rigids sustained its strong momentum, with volume growth accelerating to 13% - its highest growth in the
          last 22 quarters. Revenue growth stood at ~1%, reflecting the anniversarisation of prior pricing actions and the impact
          of price reductions undertaken at the beginning of the year to pass on the benefit of easing copra prices to consumers.
          The brand further strengthened its leadership, gaining over 570 bps market share to reach a new high of 60% volume
          market share, underscoring the brand’s formidable equity, deep consumer trust and supply chain led competitive
          advantage.
      • Value-Added Hair Oils delivered its sixth consecutive quarter of stellar growth, registering 21% value growth and
          gaining 80 bps in value market share on a MAT basis. The consistency of the performance indicates structural shift in
          the portfolio’s growth trajectory, supported by focused innovation across the mid and premium segments, benefits
          of GTM transformation led by Project SETU and sustained brand investments. Performance remained healthy across
          key franchises, with Almond Hair Oils continuing to accelerate further.
      • Saffola Edible Oils recorded 6% revenue growth during the quarter, with a high-single-digit volume decline. The
          premium range continued to grow significantly ahead of the overall portfolio led by cold-pressed oils, reinforcing our
          premiumisation agenda. We are structurally resetting the business to improve the long-term profitability of the
          franchise by rationalising the supply of select variants. Towards the end of the quarter, we responded swiftly to the
          reduction in import duties on vegetable oils by passing on the benefit to consumers through appropriate pricing
          actions.
      • Foods portfolio grew 45%, crossing an annualised revenue run rate of ₹1,500+ crore. Our sharp focus on unit economics
          and favorable mix also drove an improvement in profitability. The Saffola Foods portfolio delivered early teens
          growth, led by Saffola Oats, Muesli and Soya Chunks. Saffola Oats sustained market share and continued to retain its
          leadership position as the #1 Oats brand. True Elements, 4700BC and Cosmix continued to scale up in line with our
          aspirations recording a sequential acceleration. During the quarter, we continued to build future growth engines
          through new launches across brands, expanding into adjacencies and increasing our relevance across a wider set of
          consumer needs and occasions.
      • Premium Personal Care including our digital first brands continued its strong performance led by Beardo, Livon
          Serums and Parachute Advansed Protein Shampoo. The portfolio, comprising Premium Hair Nourishment, Hair
          Cleansing, Male Grooming and Skin Care, crossed an annualised revenue run rate of ₹500 crore and the Digital-first
          portfolio reported an annualised run rate of ~₹1,150 crore.

                    Marico – Information Update for Q2FY27 (Quarter ended September 30, 2026)
      •    The International business sustained strong growth momentum and reported 13% constant currency growth (CCG)
           and 23% revenue growth in INR terms. Bangladesh delivered 5% CCG, marking a marginal sequential improvement
           despite consumer demand softness amidst persistent high inflation and lapping a high base. We expect growth to
           gradually improve in the subsequent quarters. Vietnam continued its strong growth momentum and delivered 22%
           CCG, with healthy growth across the portfolio, and acceleration in e-commerce. MENA delivered 15% CCG, supported
           by resilient performance in the Gulf on the back of focused execution. South Africa and NCD & Exports business
           registered strong performance with 15% and 17% CCG respectively.
      •    Among key inputs, copra prices remained range-bound during the quarter at 35% below peak levels of last year. Crude-
           linked derivatives as well as Vegetable oil prices inflated further on the back of geopolitical developments and
           weather-related concerns.

      Outlook
      During the quarter, demand trends remained resilient, despite the volatile operating environment. While we remain
      optimistic about the consumption trends, we continue to closely monitor the evolving inflationary and geopolitical
      conditions.
      The strong performance in the first half of the year, with 18% consolidated revenue growth and 26% EBITDA growth,
      reflects the strength of our business model with all our growth engines viz. India Core, Digital-first and International
      businesses delivering strong performance. Supported by robust business fundamentals and disciplined execution, we
      expect to deliver teens revenue growth this year and comfortably cross the ₹15,000 Cr. revenue mark. We expect to
      achieve mid-twenties EBITDA growth in FY27. Further, India business is poised to deliver double-digit volume growth and
      International business should see accelerating constant currency growth in the second half of FY27.
      As we advance towards our Vision 2030, our focus remains clear: strengthen our core franchises, expand into adjacencies
      where we have a right to win, scale up our digital businesses profitably and further diversify our international growth
      engine. Profitable growth is central to our strategy, with a clear intent to reduce the share of commodity-linked businesses
      and progressively shift our portfolio towards categories that are more profitable. As a result, the portfolio is being

           Marico Limited – Q2FY27 Results
           Broad-Based Growth Powers Consolidated Revenue Up 13% in Q2 and 18% in H1
           India Business Sustains Double-Digit Volume Growth Momentum
           International Business delivers robust 13% CCG, 23% INR growth
           Profitability Hits Multi-Year High: EBITDA Up 27%, PAT Jumps 31%, Highest in 50 Quarters
           In Q2FY27, Consolidated Revenue from Operations grew 13% YoY to ₹3,897 crore. The India business
           delivered 10% underlying volume growth, marking another quarter of robust volume growth. The
           International business sustained healthy momentum, recording 13% constant currency growth (CCG).
           The India business continued its strong performance, with revenue up 10% YoY to ₹2,916 crore, driven by
           10% underlying volume growth, reflecting the strength of the core franchises and the continued scale-up of
           new businesses. Business fundamentals remained healthy, supported by strong offtakes and broad-based
           gains in market share and penetration. Over 95+% of the business gained or sustained market share and
           penetration on a MAT basis. Traditional and Organized Trade grew in double digits, alongside accelerated
           growth in Quick Commerce.
           Gross margin expanded ~600 bps YoY, driven by a favourable portfolio mix and softening in copra prices.
           A&P investments were up 44% YoY as we continued to invest behind strengthening the long-term equity of
           our brands and supporting growth initiatives. Backed by the strong expansion in gross margin, EBITDA
           margin expanded 200 bps to 18.2%. EBITDA and PAT grew 27%, 31% respectively, marking the strongest
           performance in the last 50 quarters.

           International Business
           International business sustained strong growth momentum and reported 13% constant currency growth
           (CCG) and 23% revenue growth in INR terms. Bangladesh delivered 5% CCG, marking a marginal sequential
           improvement despite consumer demand softness amidst persistent high inflation and lapping a high base.
           We expect growth to gradually improve in the subsequent quarters. Vietnam continued its strong growth
           momentum and delivered 22% CCG, with healthy growth across the portfolio, and acceleration in e-
           commerce. MENA delivered 15% CCG, supported by resilient performance in the Gulf on the back of
           focused execution. South Africa and NCD & Exports business registered strong performance with 15% and
           17% CCG respectively.
