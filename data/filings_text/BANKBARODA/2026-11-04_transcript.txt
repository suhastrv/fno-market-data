At the end of Q2 FY27, the Bank's global business has crossed the milestone of INR 32 lakh crore and
stands at INR 32.58 lakh crore, registering a YoY growth of 17.2 %. Our global advances have grown by
18.3% YoY, with domestic advances growing by 13.5% and international advances growing by 39.7%.

grew by 18.6%, agricultural loans by 19% and organic MSME by 21.3%. Corporate loans have grown
by 6.3% YoY. Within the retail segment, we have seen a smart growth across the portfolio with auto
loans growing by 27.8% YoY, mortgage loans by 20.3%, home loans by 14.6%, education loans by 9.9%
and personal loans by 6.8%.



In terms of the deposit growth, our total deposits have grown by 16.4% YoY, with international
deposits growing by 14.9% and domestic deposits by 16.6% YoY. The domestic CASA deposits have
grown by 10.4% and term deposits have registered a growth of 20.5% YoY. As of 30th September 2026,
the Bank's domestic credit deposit ratio has reduced by 323 bps and stands at 80.08%. Bank's domestic
CASA ratio stands at 36.37%.

With regard to our quarterly profitability metrics, our operating profit for the quarter stands at INR
11,430 crores, registering a growth of 50.9% YoY. This is the highest ever operating profit posted by
the Bank. Our net profit for the quarter grew by 35.8% YoY and stands at INR 6,532 crores. Again, this
is the highest ever quarterly profit posted by the Bank. Return on assets stands at 1.26%, improving
by 19 bps YoY. Return on equity stands at 19.28% for Q2 FY27, increasing by 363 bps YoY.
