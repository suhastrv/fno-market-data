Turning to our consolidated financial performance, revenue increased by 25% Y-o-Y in Q2
FY27 to INR1,632 crores and up 29% Y-o-Y in H1 FY27 to INR3,382 crores, supported by
healthy sales volume and improved realization.


Profitability remained under pressure both period due to elevated iron ore sourcing cost from
market and higher coal prices amid the West Asia Crisis. Additionally, Q2 FY27 witnessed the
quarter-on-quarter decline in profitability due to lower pellet realization.


EBITDA and PAT margin stood at 12% and 5.2% respectively, and 15.7% and 9.1% in H1
FY27. Margins are expected to recover from Q4 '27, with expected receipt of regulatory
permission for use of additional land for dumping of overburden and commissioning of
beneficiation plant, enabling the higher iron ore production from captive mines and use of
captive iron ore for pellet production.


Turning on operational performance, production volume recorded Y-o-Y growth across most of
the product categories in H1, with iron ore mining and galvanized fabricated products as an
exception. The iron ore mining was impacted by space constraint for dumping of overburden,
while galvanized fabricated products were affected by seasonality heavy rains.


During Q2, production continued to grow Y-o-Y across most segments. However, sequential
basis iron ore pellet production declined due to temporary closure of 2 million ton pellet plant
for uneconomical operations, while ferroalloy production was impacted by lower captive power
generation, resulting in adverse weather condition.


Considering the prevailing operations conditions, we have taken a cautious approach and revised
our FY27 production guidance for iron ore mining from 3.4 million ton to 2.5 million ton, iron
ore pellet production from 4.4 million ton to 3.5 million ton, and production across other key
segment remains on track, with ferroalloys and sponge iron achieving over 50% of annual
targets.


Our value-added product sales remained healthy growth in H1 FY27, led by pellet, sponge iron,
and rolled products. This was further supported by Y-o-Y improvement across all product
categories in both H1 and Q2, contributing healthy revenue growth. The other strategic update,
the Godawari iron ore expansion in progressing as planned.


The beneficiation plant targeted for commissioning in Q3 FY27. The plant is expected to
strengthen the captive iron ore security and improve quality of pellet, enabling the ramp-up from
Q4 and full-scale operations by FY28.


We are expecting our regulatory clearance for use of dumping ground very shortly. It is expected
anytime in next couple of days. Once the permission for use of dumping ground is allowed, then
our mining production will also increase.
