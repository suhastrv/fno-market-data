6. In respect of subsidiary ONGC Videsh Limited (OVL), the Group restructured the Offshore Area-1
   Mozambique Project through the creation of AssetCo structure involving the incorporation of MOZ
   LNG1 HoldCo Limitada (“Moz HoldCo”), an associate of the Group, and MOZ LNG1 Asset Co,
   Limitada (“AssetCo”), a subsidiary of Moz HoldCo (99.99% held by Moz HoldCo and 0.01% by the
   Operator). The restructuring aims to align the Project with customary project-finance structures while
   maintaining economic neutrality, existing participating interests, governance and risk allocation.

   Under the arrangement, the Concessionaires transfer their proportionate share of eligible project
   assets, classified under Capital Work-in-Progress (CWIP), to AssetCo in exchange for quotas and
   ancillary contributions, which are subsequently contributed to Moz HoldCo in exchange for
   corresponding interests. The Asset Transfer Agreement was signed on 10 July 2026, and the transfer
   was completed on 17 July 2026. ONGC Videsh Rovuma Limited (“OVRL”) and Beas Rovuma
   Energy Mozambique Limited (“BREML”) transferred their proportionate share of development wells,
   oil and gas facilities and related infrastructure to AssetCo, receiving quotas of USD 0.10 million each
   and an ancillary contribution of USD 948.23 million each. These quotas and ancillary contributions
   were subsequently transferred to Moz HoldCo for equivalent quotas and ancillary contributions.

   OVL management concluded that the transferred assets did not constitute a business under Ind AS
   103, Business Combinations, as they lacked an organised workforce and substantive processes. The
   transaction was therefore accounted for as a transfer of assets, not as a business combination.
   Management assessed the transaction under paragraph 30, read with paragraph 28, of Ind AS 28,
   Investments in Associates and Joint Ventures, and paragraphs 24 and 25 of Ind AS 16, Property, Plant
   and Equipment, and concluded that the exchange lacked commercial substance. Although legal title
   has transferred, Group through its components - OVRL and BREML retains substantially the same
   economic exposure as before the transfer, including their participating interests, reserve entitlements,
   fiscal terms, cost recovery rights, and future cash-call and decommissioning obligations. Accordingly,
   the investment in Moz HoldCo was measured at USD 1,659.86 million comprising of USD 0.20
   million in quotas (USD 0.10 million each) and USD 1,659.66 million OVRL - USD 827.24 million
   and BREML - USD 832.42 million) in ancillary contribution.

10. Formula used for computation of:
     a. Net worth (Total equity) = Equity share capital + Other equity + Non-Controlling Interest.
     b. Debt Equity Ratio = Total borrowings / Total equity.
     c. Interest Service Coverage Ratio = Earnings before interest, tax and exceptional item / Interest on
        borrowings (net of transfer to expenditure during construction).
     d. Debt Service Coverage Ratio = Earnings before interest, tax and exceptional item / [Interest on
        borrowings (net of transfer to expenditure during construction) + Principal repayments of Long-
        Term borrowings].
     e. Current Ratio = Current assets / Current liabilities.
     f. Long term debt to Working capital = Non-current borrowings (including current maturity of non-
        current borrowings) / Working capital (excluding current maturity of non-current borrowings).
     g. Bad debts to Accounts receivable Ratio = Bad debts / Average trade receivables.
     h. Current liability Ratio = Current liabilities / Total liabilities.
     i. Total debts to Total assets = Total borrowings / Total assets.
     j. Debtors turnover = Revenue from operations / Average trade receivables.
     k. Inventory turnover = Revenue from operations / Average inventories.
     l. Operating Margin (%) = Earnings before interest, tax and exceptional items / Revenue from
        operations.
     m. Net Profit Margin (%) = Profit for the period / Revenue from operations.
