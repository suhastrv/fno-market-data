Ahmedabad, 23rd October 2026: Adani Power Ltd. [“APL”], a part of Adani portfolio of
companies and India’s private sector largest thermal power generator, today announced
the financial results for the second quarter of financial year 2026-27.



                                                                                          1

Commenting on the results, Mr. S B Khyalia, CEO of Adani Power Limited, said, “Adani
Power continues to grow from strength to strength, achieving newer milestones in
profitability and project execution. As India’s leading private thermal power producer, our
vision is focused on ensuring the timely availability of high quality and reliable power
supply to meet the country’s growing baseload requirements. The primacy of domestic
coal-based power in meeting India’s long-term power needs has become clearer every day
in light of geopolitical events and climate vagaries. We are proud to be the leaders in
capacity addition to address the need of 100 GW new thermal power capacities by 2032.
We are ensuring, with the determined support of our experienced teams and capable
partners, that India will always have access to the power supply it needs round-the-clock
to meet its growth aspirations.”

Power market update:
During Q2 FY27, India’s power demand grew by 11.5% at 502 BU as compared to Q2 FY26,
driven by rapid economic growth and a weak monsoon. Growing demand resulted in 11%
higher power generation year-on-year to reach 536 Billion Units (“BU”), which coupled with
lower hydropower potential resulted in higher contribution in power supply from Coal and
Lignite-fired power generation at 66% as compared to 65% in Q2 FY26. Coal and Lignite-
based power accounted for 73% of the incremental power generation in the quarter.
Aggregate Plant Load Factor (“PLF”) of coal-based power plants in India rose to 68% in Q2
FY27, as compared to 62% in Q2 FY26.

Peak power demand reached 270 GW in Q2 FY27, up 18% from 230 GW recorded in the
previous year. Large states like Uttar Pradesh and Maharashtra accounted for 27 GW of
peak demand each during Q2 FY27, followed by Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, and Karnataka with
23 GW, 20 GW, and 16 GW respectively.

During Q2 FY27, the Day-Ahead Market Clearing Price on the Indian Energy Exchange rose
by 46% year-on-year to Rs. 5.7/unit. The Real-Time Market Clearing Price also increased by
49% year-on-year to Rs. 5.2/unit.
