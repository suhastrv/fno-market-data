         NOTES TO THE STANDALONE UNAUDITED FINANCIAL RESULTS:

  1. The standalone unaudited financial results ("the statement") of Apollo Hospitals
     Enterprise Limited ("the Company") for the three and six months ended
     September 30, 2026 have been reviewed by the Audit Committee and approved by
     the Board of Directors of the Company at their meetings held on November 3, 2026
     and November 4, 2026 respectively. The statutory auditors, Deloitte Haskins &
     Sells LLP have expressed an unmodified review conclusion on the financial results
     for the three and six months ended September 30, 2026.

  2. The standalone unaudited financial results have been prepared in accordance with
     the Indian Accounting Standards ("Ind AS") as prescribed under Section 133 of the
     Companies Act, 2013 ("the Act") read with relevant rules issued thereunder and in
     terms of Regulation 33 of SEBI (Listing Obligation and Disclosure requirements)
     Regulations, 2015.

  3.   The Company's business activity primarily falls within a single reportable business
       segment namely "Healthcare     Services" and operates primarily        in India and
       accordingly does not have any additional disclosure to be made under Ind AS 108-
       "Segment Reporting".

 4. The Board at its meeting held on June 30, 2025, subject to necessary approvals
    considered and approved a Composite Scheme of Arrangement amongst, the
    Company (AHEL), Apollo Healthco Limited ("Transferor Company 1" or "AHL") -
    Keimed Private Limited ("Transferor Company 2"), and Apollo Healthtech Limited
    ("Resultant   Company")     and their  respective   shareholders and creditors
    ("Scheme"),   in accordance with the provisions of Sections 230-232 and other
    applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, the SEBI (LODR) Regulations,
    2015 read with SEBI Master Circular dated June 20, 2023 (SEBI/HO/CFD/POD-
    2/P/CIR/2023/93), and other applicable rules, regulations, and circulars issued by
    regulatory authorities.

       The Scheme inter alia provides for demerger of identified business undertaking (as
       defined in the scheme)      primarily representing the Omni channel pharmacy
       distribution business and digital health platform business of the Company into the
       Resultant Company; the amalgamation of "Transferor Company 1" with and into
       the Resultant Company; the amalgamation of "Transferor Company 2" with and
       into the Resultant Company; and the consequent listing of the equity shares of the
       Resultant Company on the National Stock Exchange of India Limited and Bombay
       Stock Exchange Limited and admission to trading.

       National Stock Exchange of India Limited and Bombay Stock Exchange Limited
       have issued letters dated December 23,2025 and December 24, 2025 respectively,
       under regulation 37 of SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure    Requirements)
       Regulations, 2015. Based on the directions of the Hon'ble Company Law Tribunal,
       meetings of the Secured        Creditors,  Unsecured   Creditors and the Equity
       Shareholders of the Company were convened on June 24, 2026 with requisite
       approvals being obtained for implementation      of the Scheme and status being
       updated to the Company Law Tribunal. Subsequent to the receipt of such approvals,
       the Company has filed a petition before the National Company Law Tribunal seeking
       sanction of the scheme.

EXPANSION INNOVATION AND PARTNERSHIPS
During Q2 FY27, Apollo continued to advance its healthcare expansion, technology and innovation agenda
through capacity creation, new models of patient access and strategic collaborations.


1.   Capacity Expansion: Apollo continued to ramp up operations at its Financial District hospital in Hyderabad,
     Sonarpur hospital in Kolkata and Sarjapur hospital in Bengaluru, with its Gurugram facility on track for
     commissioning in Q3 FY27. The Group's broader expansion programme envisages adding over 5,800 beds
     over the next five years, strengthening its presence across key markets.

2.   Always Open, Always Here: Launched on August 18, 2026, the Group-wide initiative extends routine and
     planned healthcare services to Sundays, including outpatient consultations, diagnostics, preventive health
     checks, follow-ups and scheduled procedures, improving access and convenience for patients.

3.   Advancing Critical Care: Apollo Hospitals and Medtronic inaugurated an 11-bed ICU of the Future at Apollo
     Hospitals, Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad, on July 16, 2026. The facility integrates advanced acute-care
     monitoring technologies with clinical expertise, creating an environment for technology-enabled critical
     care and clinical innovation.

4.   Research and Workforce Development: Apollo Knowledge and BITS Pilani signed an MoU on September
     29, 2026, to collaborate on healthcare research, education and workforce development through joint
     research initiatives and work-integrated learning programmes for healthcare professionals and
     managers.

CORPORATE SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY
During Q2 FY27, Apollo Foundation continued to advance its community healthcare and development
initiatives. Apollo Foundation – Total Health reached 2.13 lakh direct beneficiaries through AHEL CSR-funded

Basic Life Support (BLS) and First Aid training, strengthening community-level emergency preparedness.
