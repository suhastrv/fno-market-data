              2.   Second, we secured our first order at Chester Hall Precision Engineering, our aerospace
                   business in the UK, since the acquisition.


                   During the quarter, Chester Hall secured an extension order booking for track beams on a
                   legacy program for one of the largest aircraft platforms of the world's largest aircraft OEM.
                   To give some context, track beams are safety-critical structural components of the engine
                   nacelle, which is the structure surrounding an aircraft's jet engine.


                   The nacelle helps channel airflow generated by the engine's fan blades, which accounts for
                   the significant majority of the engine's thrust. More importantly, track beams form an
                   integral part of the thrust reverser system, which helps slow down the aircraft after landing
                   by redirecting airflow forward.


                   These beams guide the movement of the thrust reverser panels as they open and close, while
                   also providing structural support to the engine enclosure. Their design and manufacturing
                   requires deep material expertise, precision engineering, and extensive load analysis.
                   Importantly, the contract also comes with improved commercial terms, including price
                   increases, which further strengthens the economics of the program.


              3.   Lastly, subsequent to the quarter, we also entered into a license and technical assistance
                   agreement with a Japanese player called Yamato Co, Ltd. Yamato has established expertise
                   in filtration products and in converting intake manifolds from metal to resin. Under the
                   agreement, Yamato has granted exclusive rights to manufacture and sell three product lines
                   in India, including air filters, oil filters, and resin air intake manifolds for both automotive
                   and industrial equipment OEMs.


                   Resin intake manifolds are part of the industry's wider move towards lighter components,
                   and we see demand for them across two-wheelers, passenger cars, as well as industrial
                   applications. Yamato will support us across the full product cycle, covering design,
                   development, testing, and manufacturing.


                   This gives us access to proven technology and manufacturing know-how, and allows us to
                   offer our customers, a locally developed and manufactured product backed by an
                   established Japanese technology partner. Strategically, this is a natural extension of what
                   we already do.


                   We have been supplying filters and plastic components to two leading Japanese OEMs for
                   the past couple of years, and this partnership also enables us to expand in the aftermarket
