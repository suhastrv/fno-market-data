Note on Damas Acquisition
•   The Damas acquisition, completed in Q 4FY 26, has been structured in 2 tranches .

•   Tranche - 1 of the acquisition of 67% equity stake was completed in Q 4FY 26 for a consideration of ₹1,190 crores . As part of the
    arrangement, the balance 33% equity stake is linked to milestones and would be completed post CY 29

•   On the acquisition date, an estimated (option) liability of ₹1,668 crores was accounted       for Tranche - 2 in line with applicable
    accounting standards

•   Ind - AS 109 requires fair valuation of this option liability at the end         of every   quarter   based   on the evaluation     of
    probable business performance in the 4 year period from CY 26 to CY 29

•   Post acquisition, due to the volatile geopolitical situation in the Middle East region, the business performance of Damas has been
    impacted for CY 26. Accordingly, based on current assessment, the (option) liability of Tranche - 2 has been reduced by an amount
    of ₹671 crores . Leading to a corresponding non - operational credit in the reported financial statements

•   It is to be noted that as the Ind - AS 109 standard requires this fair valuation at every reporting date . We expect that based on the
    probable performance assessment in the coming quarters till CY 29, corresponding accounting adjustments could lead to an
    increase or decrease in the (option) liability including forex revaluation impact, if any
