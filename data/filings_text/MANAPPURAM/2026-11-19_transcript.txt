Elevated domestic gold prices have increased the collateral value of household gold holdings

small traders, and farmers, a gold loan is the quickest form of secured credit and a way to
access liquidity without selling an asset of financial, personal, or family significance.

lenders. Higher gold prices also require lenders to remain disciplined. Our emphasis is on
responsible customer acquisition, appropriate loan-to-value levels, accurate appraisal, secure
custody of pledged gold, and timely monitoring of accounts. We explain loan terms and
auction procedures clearly to our customers. These practices protect our customers as well as
the quality of our portfolio.
