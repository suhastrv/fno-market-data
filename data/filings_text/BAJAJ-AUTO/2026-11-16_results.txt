                                    Press Release: Q2 FY27 Results (Standalone)

 A MILESTONE QUARTER IN A CHALLENGING OPERATING CONTEXT
                  Records on All Counts (Vc:,lumes I Revenue I Profits I Cash)

Quarter Highlights
 • Revenue registered its first ever ~20,000 crore print, doubling in 3 years , as it grew 34% YoY with new highs on vehicles and spares
   o A defining feature of the performance was its breadth - with growth exceeding 20% YoY across powertrains, markets and vehicle
     categories {2W /3W); record volumes were further bolstered by judicious pricing, richer sales mix and favourable currency realisation

• Strong and consistent profit delivery, as EBITOA and Profit after Tax surpass the ~4.000 crore and ~3.000 crore marks (up 35%+ YoY)
  o Margins held firm at 20.9% amidst another quarter of volatility and hyper-inflation; underscoring the disciplined and dynamic P&L
      management, as currency tailwind, operating leverage and cost control made up for the partial recovery of input inflation through pricing

• Domestic business posted record quarterly revenues, registering a robust 30% YoY growth on a solid show from both 2Ws and 3Ws
  o While ICE maintained its steady growth, it was the electric portfolio which spearheaded the growth, closing in at a record ~200k units
    (vs. 82k in 02'26) despite navigating a significantly constrained supply situation, and now constituting one-third of domestic revenues

• Exports set the pace with another landmark quarter (up 40%+ YoY), raising the bar on volumes despite intermittenllogistical disruptions
  o Africa nearly doubled its billing over the base quarter to reach a new high surpassing its FY21 quarterly peak, while LATAM sustained
    its strong momentum to register its best-€ver sales yet again; a buoyant quarter that was boosted by commercial vehicles at >100k units

• Domestic motorcycles continued to strengthen its performance, with double-digit sports segment growth and a broader portfolio refresh
  o Encouraging early traction in Pulsar portfolio on the back of recent launches, with sequential market share gains in its sports segment
    led by the N and NS series models; further upgrades in the offing to step-up competitiveness, with a continued thrust on premiumisation

• KTM +Triumph domestic revenues at a new record, up 45% YoY, maintaining its strong growth trajectory with volumes >40,000 units
  o KTM turned in a stellar quarter with robust sales on both Adventure and Duke bikes while Triumph gained further traction with its modern
    classics line-up although setback by supply unavailability; wider reach through K+ T stores (11 0+ towns) aiding the contemporary lineup

• Commercial Vehicles scaled a new peak on quarterly revenues and volumes (>160k units), turbo charged by the electric portfolio
  o Pole position maintained in e3Ws (L5) as volumes and revenues more than doubled YoY, with capacity being augmented further to serve
    unmet demand; Riki extended its footprin t to ~185 cities and now leads thee-rick (L3) lithium-ion segment, with plans to broaden its play

• Chetak delivered its biggest ever quarter, as volumes more than doubled YoY (outpacing industry growth by 2x) on a relatively soft base
  o Revenues crossed the t2,000 crore mark and profitability continued to improve, even as the quarter was marked by.severe supply issues
    as demand outstripped an exceptionally lean pipeline; production is being ramped up to improve fulfilment for both domestic and exports

• Focus on cash generation led to record quarterly Free Cash Flow accretion of -~4.000 crore and full conversion of H1 FY27 PAT to cash
  o Balance sheet remains healthy, with surplus funds of ~t15,500 crore, after spending on capex, continued growth investments and
     returning ~t10,000 crore to shareholders through dividends and buyback - while retaining ample capacity to fund strategic priorities
