    • Caplin continues steady and consistent growth in Latin America across all geographies and segments.

       submission batches are planned in the next 12 months, for LatAm, US and EU markets.
    • Caplin’s API unit in Vizag for General Category products completed scale up for 6 APIs, all to be used for
