The government has also announced a special support to address the exceptional increase in
Sulphur and Sulphuric acid prices, which has significantly impacted the cost of phosphatic
fertilizers. During the year, government launched the Framework for Fertilizer Sale system on
a pilot basis across select districts.


These initiatives are very positive from the perspective of improving transparency and
promoting efficient fertilizer usage. The initial implementation has also highlighted certain
practical challenges, including longer purchase process for farmers, access to digital
infrastructure, and accommodating farmer choice and brand preferences.


Industry has shared its feedback with the Department of Fertilizers, and we are hopeful that
these operational aspects will be suitably addressed before the framework becomes a reality. In
AgChem, we continue to see regulatory tightening, including restriction around certain
molecules like Carbosulfan and Paraquat. However, impact on Coromandel is not significant.


With respect to non-subsidy businesses, restriction has been imposed by certain states on the
sale of non-subsidy fertilizers by fertilizer marketers. Industry has been engaging with the
authorities in the state of U.P., Bihar, and Maharashtra, and we are hopeful that an appropriate
framework can be evolved that protects farmer interests while continuing to encourage
balanced nutrition and innovation.


During the quarter, Andhra Pradesh has announced a major program to develop Rayalaseema
as a global horticulture and food processing hub, and significant expansion is planned in
horticulture acreage. Given our strong presence in the region, and our new granulation plant
coming on stream in the near future, this augurs very well for Coromandel. We see this as a
positive long-term opportunity across our various segments, nutrients, crop protection,
specialty nutrients, especially through our retail platform.


Coming specifically to Q2 performance, as I mentioned, it was a very challenging quarter.


We sharpened our focus on differentiated offerings. We launched 10 new products across
nutrients and crop protection segments. Reasonably, we managed the working capital well.
Revenue grew by 6% to INR10,348 crores; however, EBITDA declined 18%, mainly due to
compression in NPK fertilizer margins during this quarter.


Also due to deficit in key markets on rainfall and higher input prices not adequately
compensated by Kharif NBS rates, our performance across the value chain got impacted. In the
fertilizer business, volume uptake was impacted due to monsoon and price differential.

quarter was down significantly by 30% to 10 lakh tons, and consumption also declined 33%. It
was a conscious call taken by the company to moderate the primary sale and production to
manage the working capital. by closely tracking consumption, production and supplies. With
the government announcing the new NBS rates for the Rabi season, we do expect that part of
the benefits will be passed on in correcting the MRPs, which can revive consumption during
the Rabi season.
