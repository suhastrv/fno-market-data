                                                Outlook


❑ Raw material inflation and supply chain volatility continue to weigh on gross margins while competitive
   intensity remains elevated, keeping the near-term operating environment challenging


❑ We will respond with differentiation – deepening consumer connect, investing behind brand building and
   innovation fueling unique product propositions – to drive the demand momentum further as well as
   deliver sustained profitable growth


❑ Key priorities for Q3FY27 will include:


❖ Capitalize on festive and wedding season demand with disciplined execution across markets
❖ Sustain investment in innovation and brand-building, leveraging the newly commissioned VAE capacity to
   scale differentiated in-house formulations
❖ Drive calibrated pricing and cost efficiencies to improve margins while safeguarding demand momentum
