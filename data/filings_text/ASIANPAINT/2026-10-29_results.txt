❖ Highlights for Q2 FY’27

•   Decorative Business (India) delivered volume and value growth of 6.4% and 14.5% respectively
•   Domestic coatings business delivered value growth of 16.4%, with continued strength in industrial
    coatings aiding the decorative growth trajectory
•   International business Net Sales grew 29.5% (23.7% in constant currency), with strong growth across
    all regions
•   Commissioned Phase 1 of the VAE plant at Dahej, which will pave way for new wave of innovation
•   Further round of staggered price increases being taken in the Decorative business to counter sustained
    raw material inflation


Mumbai, October 29, 2026: Asian Paints today announced their financial results for the quarter ended
September 30, 2026.

“We delivered a resilient performance in Q2, a seasonally softer quarter, sustaining the momentum of the last
few quarters. Overall, the domestic coatings business grew by 16.4% with a volume growth of 6.8%. The
Decorative business delivered a value growth of 14.5% with a volume growth of 6.4% and the Industrial coatings
business continued its impressive run with a value growth of 31.8%.

Our International business grew 29.5%, with strong performance across all regions. Middle East region also
registered robust growth despite servicing challenges posed by the ongoing geopolitical situation. In Home
Décor, we saw improved scale in part of the business, and we continue to take steps to strengthen its overall
performance trajectory.

The operating environment this year has been marked by sharp raw material inflation and material availability

About Asian Paints Limited:
Asian Paints is India’s leading paint and decor company and ranked among the top 8 coatings companies in
the world with a consolidated turnover of ₹ 35,516 crore (₹ 355 billion) in FY’26 and with a market
capitalization of approx. ₹ 2,305* billion. Asian Paints along with its subsidiaries have operations in 14
countries across the world with 25 paint manufacturing facilities, servicing consumers in over 60 countries
through Asian Paints, Apco Coatings, Asian Paints Berger, Asian Paints Causeway, SCIB Paints, Taubmans and
Kadisco Asian Paints. Asian Paints also offers a wide range of Home Décor products and is an emerging strong
player in the Home Improvement and Décor space in India.
