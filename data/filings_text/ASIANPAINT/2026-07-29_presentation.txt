Outlook for Q2 FY27



  The renewed conflict has intensified volatility across raw material costs and supply chain logistics, keeping
    the near-term operating environment uncertain


  Against this backdrop, our priority is to sustain growth momentum and meet competitive intensity
    through consumer connect, emotional marketing and technology innovation


  Key priorities for Q2FY27 will include:
      Execute with speed and discipline across markets to sustain momentum
      Step up investment in innovation and brand-building to deepen consumer relevance, sharpen
        differentiation and strengthen market salience
      Drive cost efficiencies to absorb external pressures, protect margins and safeguard demand
