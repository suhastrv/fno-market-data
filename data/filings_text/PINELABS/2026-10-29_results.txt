Press Release | Q2 FY27 Performance



Pine Labs Grows Revenue 24% YoY to a 5-Quarter High; PAT Grows
 6X; Operating Cash Flow Doubles to 350 Cr; Continues to Evolve
    Product and Technology Stack Across Every Business Line


29 October 2026 | Gurugram, India: Q2 FY27 was the quarter the Pine Labs platform
showed what compounding looks like in practice. Revenue grew 24% YoY to 2806 Cr
(the fastest in five quarters), but the deeper signal lies underneath. The Group now
processes ~25 million transactions a day across businesses, up ~22% YoY. 29%+ of
DITP GTV is now being monetizedlinked with volume, vs. 15% a year ago. 14% of this
quarter's revenue came from products that didn't exist two years ago. 15% came from
markets outside India. Four separate growth engines—none dependent on the other, all
firing. Beneath the top-line growth, each layer of the platform is doing more work than
the one beforeit.
Q2 was an all-round quarter—growth across acceptance and issuing, 15% of revenues
from global markets, and 14% from products launched only in the last two years," said
Amrish Rau, CEO, Pine Labs. 'Pine Labs has been EBITDA-positive for a long time. The
way we look at the business is to find the right balance between investing in new
products, new ideas, and new regions—while continuing to be strongly EBITDA positive.
20%+ EBITDA margins and 21-23% revenue growth is the fine balance we aim to strike."
As the platform scales, the benefits are increasingly translating into stronger platform
economics. Al is fundamentally changing how Pine Labs is built and operated, with
~89% of code changes now contributed by Al agents. The focus remains on reinvesting
these efficiency gains into new products, new regions, and new categories while
maintaining a strong EBITDA profile—building a larger, more durable commerce
platform that can sustain profitable growth over the long term.
Pine Labs expanded Adjusted EBITDA to 2155 Cr (+27% YoY, 19.2% margin) and PAT grew
6X to239 Cr (PBT: 262 Cr vs 21l Cr). Operating Cash Flow doubled to 350 Cr;
Contribution Margin grew 18% YoY to 587 Cr. Platform GTV reached ~$46.5 Bn (24.45
lakh Cr) across 229 crore transactions. Net Cash balance of 22,345 Cr as of 30-Sep-26.

Where the growth came from
Instore held its groundin Enterprise and gained decisively in mid-market, where the DCP
base grew 49% YoY. The overall DCP base is up 17% YoY to 22.3 lakh. On top of this
installed base, Pine Labs is sweating assets harder—Flow, Affordability and Transaction
Processing GTV grew 52%# YoY, UPI GTV 77%+ YoY. A 10 lakh QR soundbox rollout over
the medium term will carry UPI acceptance deeper into retail and premium retail
merchants.
Online payments grew 45%+ YoY with GTV up 140% YoY, as the business pushedinto
agentic commerce, cross-border, SMB, and government corridors. At Global Fintech Fest
2026, Pine Labs demonstrated the industry’s first live autonomous agentic transaction
on BigBasket. Issuing grew 31%+ YoY overall (India +40%), with new value pools scaling
fast across employee benefits, entertainment and corporate gifting, gaming, and
mobility—Bharat Yatra on track for ~5 million tripsin FY27.
Internationalis becoming a franchise of its own. SEA revenue grew ~40% YoY,
international DCPs scaled close to 1lakh in Southeast Asia (2.7X YoY), and the UAE
business crossed ~1lakh daily transactions. Pine Labs operates across 22+ markets
with credit processingin 17+, and is extending the Malaysia playbook to the UAE,
Philippines, and Singapore.

Building the nextcycle
Pine Labs used HI FY27 to accelerate investment into the next wave of commerce. At
Global Fintech Fest 2026, the company unveiled a wider set of new products across
issuing, payments, and agentic commerce. Pine Labs' long-term engagement
partnership with OpenAl, alongside investments in agentic commerce, information
security, and international expansion, underpins the next cycle of product velocity.
The UPI MDR framework (P2M , notified for transactions above 22,000, is a sizeable tailwind.
With 70%+ of DCP transactions already running on UPI and 65%+ of UPI GTV driven by
tickets above 22,000, Pine Labs sits squarely in the window the framework was designed
to monetize. The company is on track to average ~234-35K Cr of monthly UPI GTV in FY28,
with an average ticket of ~21,400. Alongside, Pine Labs is stepping up brand investments to
positionitself as the default commerce infrastructure for the global south.
“The opportunity set in front of us is widening, not narrowing,” said Rau. "A wider set of
new products this quarter, 10 lakh soundboxes committed over the medium term, agentic
commerce live on a top Indian marketplace, and a UPI monetization window opening
up—this is the time to build."
