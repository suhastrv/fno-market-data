In recent weeks, we have secured significant orders across both government and private sector

while maintaining discipline on execution, customer diversification, and returns.


The outlook remains strong, and the recently announced tender for 5,800 BOBRN wagons
further strengthens the industrial opportunity.


We are making significant progress in building an integrated railwheel manufacturing platform
through Jupiter Tatravagonka Railwheel Factory Limited, where we have entered into a strategic
partnership with Italy-based Lucchini RS, who brings specialized expertise in railwheels, axles,
and forged components, along with advanced manufacturing technology and international
market experience. This partnership will strengthen our technical capabilities and support the
development of an integrated Indian railwheel platform, spanning forging, machining, and
finished wheelsets, with opportunities for import substitution and exports.


As part of the engagement and arrangement, Lucchini RS, along with SIMEST, an Italian
government financial institute, has been allotted a combined 25% stake in JTRWF against the
investment of approximately INR 290 crore. Our railways portfolio is already broad and well-
established. Complete passenger mobility solutions were the natural next step, and that's the
opportunity we have now moved to capture.


I am pleased to share that we have signed an MOU in October with PESA, Poland's largest train
and metro manufacturer, to explore cooperation in passenger rail vehicle development,
manufacturing, and deployment across regional intercity and urban transport markets.


The collaboration combines PESA's passenger rail technology and European experience with
Jupiter Wagons' manufacturing scale, Indian Railways relationship and capabilities across
rolling stock components, castings and wheelsets. It provides a framework to explore
opportunities across metro, semi-high speed, and high-speed rail segments, including component
localization in India and other markets. With this, we will progress our offering from key
components and parts into comprehensive, end-to-end offering in passenger mobility too.


Our energy storage business is gaining traction, supported by the growing adoption of renewable
energy and the increasing need for grid stability and flexible energy management solutions.
