Subject: Clarification letter

Re: Your email dated June 2, 2025, regarding Clarification for Financial results - INFY

We wish to inform you that, the clause being referred is under Reg 33 (3)(e) is replicated below for
reference:

The listed entity shall also submit the audited or limited reviewed financial results in respect of the last
quarter along-with the results for the entire financial year, with a note stating that the figures of last quarter
are the balancing figures between audited figures in respect of the full financial year and the published
year-to-date figures upto the third quarter of the current financial year

Our response – As per the SEBI clause a note has to be given stating that the last quarter figures (Q4) is
the balancing figure of year to date figures for the full year and the figures published till Q3 of that year.
Infosys presents audited quarterly financial results to the stock exchange every quarter. Every quarterly
result is based on audited financials for that quarter and year to date figures including the last quarter (Q4).
The Q4 results are not balancing figures rather they are based on audited financial statements for the
quarter ended March 31, 2025. Accordingly, since the note mentioned is Reg 33 3(e) is not applicable to
the company the company has not provided the same in its filing with the stock exchange. Also this practice
has been followed by the company consistently year on year.
