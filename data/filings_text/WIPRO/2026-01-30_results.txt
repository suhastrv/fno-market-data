We have received an email from (“NSE”) requesting clarification from Wipro Limited
(“Company”) regarding the following matter:

This is with respect to a Outcome of Board Meeting-Financial Results submitted to the
Exchange dated 16-Jan-2026 of WIPRO: Wipro Limited. Following deficiency/ non submission
have/has been observed in your result, you are requested to kindly clarify on below mentioned
points.

1. Financial results submitted in XBRL with discrepancies - In consolidated financial results,
mismatch in Profit before tax figures

Response:

   1. The Company respectfully submits that the consolidated financial results are prepared
      in accordance with Schedule III of Companies Act, 2013. The Company has certain
      investments which are classified as associates under the relevant accounting standard.
      The profit and loss from such associates are accounted under equity methods and are
      included in the profit before tax numbers as prescribed under schedule III.
   2. The XBRL format (as published) captures the profit and loss from associates at net
      profit level and not as part of profit before tax.
   3. The Company has also added the below note to this effect in its XBRL filling.

       Note: 1 Profit before Tax excludes Share of net profit loss of associate and joint venture
       accounted for using equity method
