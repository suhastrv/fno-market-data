Now coming to asset quality. We saw improvement in asset quality across each of our business
segments except gold loan. Our gross Stage-2 assets spiked by Rs. 1,266 crores, which is entirely
attributable to gold loan Stage-2 spike by similar levels and is temporal in nature, also given the

Following the RBI's revised gold loan guideline with effect from April 2026, which required
accrued interest to be factored into LTV ceiling for bullet loans, most major gold loan lenders
moved towards shorter tenure products. We similarly introduced monthly and bi-monthly
repayment frequency loans in Q1 FY '27. These shorter cycles led to technical delinquencies
driven by repayment convenience remittance rather than borrower ability or willingness to pay.
Shorter loans simply tripped overdue thresholds sooner. This was a widely reported industry
experience. We have already discontinued these schemes and reverted to standard longer tenure
structures.


The increase in reported GNPA in gold loan particularly reflects product-level repayment
dynamics and our calibrated approach to collateral enforcement rather than a corresponding
increase in expected credit losses. Importantly, the underlying collateral coverage and
historically demonstrated recovery performance continue to provide substantial protection
against principal loss.


As part of our transition to the revised regulatory framework, we introduced certain shorter-
tenure repayment structures during Q1 FY '27. While these products were designed to facilitate
more frequent repayment discipline, the change in repayment frequency created some friction
for customers accustomed to conventional bullet repayment structure, resulting in high technical
delinquencies.
