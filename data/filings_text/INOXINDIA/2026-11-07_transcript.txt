required thermal performance and extremely high flow rates while ensuring complete separation
between oxidizers and fuel systems.


Manufacturing these subcoolers involves precision deep hole drilling, automated orbital TIG
welding, hydraulic tube expansion, controlled tube bending, and stringent cleaning procedures.
Quality assurance includes helium leak testing, borescopic inspection, and thermal cycling.
